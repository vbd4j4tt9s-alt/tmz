# R157: the Bag without green, and without the hint line

Owner: "dont make this green and remove that text saying click to hold and so on". The owner approved **Option 2** of `docs/proposals/R156/bag_look.md`: no green at all in the Bag, the hint line gone. This release puts it in `src/`. The picture is `bag157.png` (PC and landscape phone: at rest, an item dragged over the Bag, the Discard bin targeted, the discard popup), drawn from the real `src` by `preview/run_bag157_png.sh` (R156's `run_bag_look.sh` pointed at `src`, one variant). It is an approximation (a mock plus Chromium, not a Studio screenshot).

## What changed

* **Colours** (`Hotbar.client.lua`, `InventoryPanel155.lua`, `DiscardDialog155.lua`; the edits of R156's `make_option2_src.py`, line for line):

| What | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet, rarity dropdown panel, discard popup (`C.Sheet`) | #142E23 | #1F2246 |
| Cards, tabs, rarity button, To Bag / x, popup picture box and 1 / - / + / All (`C.Tile`) | #254C39 | #30366A |
| Selected tab, Hold button, drag ghost (`C.TileOn`) | #41784C | #4A5599 |
| Search box, count, popup count box (`C.Well`) | #0E2119 | #192041 |
| Sheet rim, header rule, popup rim | #93FF45 | #6A90E1 |
| Header band | #41634D | #424A80 |
| Lit colour: drop highlight, slot outlines, picked ring, "Moving ..." text (`C.Green` is now `C.Lit`) | #93FF45 | #EAF0FF |
| "Keep it" | #466E56 | #424C8A |

* **The drop highlight** is a thin light rim: a 2 px #EAF0FF rim at 85% opaque and a faint brighten (14% on a slot or the Bag button, 8% on the sheet), where it was a 55% lime flood and a 3 px lime rim.
* **The Discard bin stays red** (button #7A2C34, highlight #FF6060, the popup's Discard #C44048).
* **The hint line is removed** on PC, phone and gamepad: the `Hint` label, the line that showed and hid it in `Layout` (Hotbar) and `Layout` / `paintPick` (`InventoryPanel155`). The bottom bar is the count next to Discard, where it was (8 px left of Discard); the picked-item bar ("Moving ...", Hold, To Bag, x) is not the hint and stays.
* **Nothing shared changed.** `GardenTheme` (Mint stays the game's green) and `GardenMenuStyle.Panel` (every other menu's green trim, header band and rule) are untouched: the Bag recolours its own trim, band and rule after the call. `C` is the Hotbar's own table. `C.Lit` also colours the hotbar slot's "pending" ring and the `/test hotbar` log title (they used `C.Green`).
* **Left alone on purpose:** rarity colours (Common grey-green line, the "Uncommon" word), white selection outlines, the amber / red count and FULL badge, the popup's amber warning, the new-pack rainbow.
* **Hotbar main chunk: 174 of 180 registers** (no top-level local added); lines 1 and 2 (the load guard) and the held-name / pity-bar lines are not touched.

## Tests

* `tests/run_bag157.sh` (new, registered at the end of line 6 of `tools/tests/run_all_suites.sh`): static checks (no `Hint` label or hint words, no lime or old green in the Bag's scripts, the new palette, the thin rim, Discard red, shared palette untouched, load guard, register count) and `test_bag157.luau` on a PC, a landscape phone and a portrait phone (the only sheet that ever showed the phone hint): no hint in any state, the count 8 px left of Discard, every colour above, the drop highlight is the light rim on the sheet / a slot / the Bag button, the bin lights red, the slot outlines / picked ring / "Moving" text are #EAF0FF, no Color3 anywhere on the Bag or popup is #93FF45 or an old green after any state, other menus still get their green. Run against the old `src` it fails on the hint and every colour.
* `R155/tests/test_no_tooltip156.luau` section 1b used to check the hint's text ("Click to hold • ..."): it now checks that the hint is gone (the R156 note "no test reads the hint" missed it).
* The rest of the Bag's behaviour (drag, tap-tap, discard, search, the count) is the R155 suites, unchanged and passing.
