# R157 HUD: the three approved R156 previews, built

Picture: `hud157.png` (drawn from this branch's real `src/` with the R156 preview scripts: `sh preview/run_hud157_preview.sh <scratch dir>`). It is approximate, not a
Studio screenshot. Tests: `sh tests/run_hud157.sh <scratch dir>`. The runner is registered on line 6 of `tools/tests/run_all_suites.sh`.

## What was built
1. **Pity bars v2** (`pity_bars_v2.md`): only shade 1 "Fresh" is left ("Deep" is gone). NORMAL is clover green and EVENT is gold with a clover-green rim. The
   clover (CloverPassImage153) on a dark disc replaces the diamonds. The bars sit 8 px over the slots on a PC and 6 px on a phone (5 px under a thin 16 px phone bar).
   The held item's name and traits rows now sit right above the bars. They are hidden on 320 x 568, 360 x 640 and 360 x 740 portrait. The event notice and tag use a
   gold star ⭐. The lucky pop, its shine and its flash use the bar's own colours. BONUS ROLL, the SKIP pill and the lucky tag all follow the new layout.
2. **Reveal fixes** (`reveal_fixes.md`): only Secret, Cosmic and King have a SKIP pill. Common..Mythic have no `SkipFrom`, and click / tap / Enter / B / R2 still
   collect once the result shows. In a story scene the pill sits in the safe area's corner, 14 px in, and only moves for a thumb control that is really on screen.
   Common..Mythic cards and the compact Secret+ card are fitted (`RarePullCard.FitBand`, `RarePullRules.FitLayout`) between BASE / TRACK (`HudLayout.TravelBottom`,
   which TravelButtons also draws by) and the top of the NEW bars and name rows. "click to collect!" / "tap to collect!" sits right under the name, in the story result
   too. The card publishes its rows, and the lucky tag follows them.
3. **DAILY + INVITE in the MENU wheel** (`menu_wheel_daily.md`): a five-option half circle, as in the .md table (MENU moves as listed). BASE / TRACK have the top bar
   row to themselves, with no DAILY / INVITE squares and no `m.Travel`. DAILY's badge sits on its option's top-right corner, like INDEX's. When the wheel is closed,
   MENU shows one red "!" (`NotifyBadge151` Alert) if a daily or Index reward is waiting. The INVITE hint is on its own layer, so it is not clipped. The friend "+%"
   chip shows on INVITE, and beside MENU while the wheel is closed. Gamepad selection links are explicit while the wheel is open. The fallbacks: smaller options under
   ~280 px, and DAILY / INVITE in a row past SHOP under ~230 px.

## Deviations (and why)
- **Name rows** are at most 240 px wide and centred over the hotbar, not as wide as it. Their text is centred, and full width ran into the balances and status on small windows.
- **The name-hiding rule** is written out as "portrait and narrower than 370 and shorter than 780 px". That hides the rows on 360 x 640 / 360 x 740, as approved, and
  shows them on 375 x 667 and up.
- **PC balances**: the wheel's lower options (INVITE) reach into the balances' corner on 1366 x 768 / 1280 x 720 / 800 x 600. There the three rows get shorter (down to
  16 px) and stay in the corner. Only tiny windows (700 x 400 and smaller) move them to R113's other spots.
- **PC 640 x 360 fallback ring**: its first option is pulled in to radius 88 so it stays off the owner tools tile.
- **BONUS ROLL** slides sideways along its row when an option of the open wheel takes its spot (portrait phones), instead of going to the screen's edge.
- **SKIP on a Secret+ in-place card on tiny PC windows** (640 x 360, 420 x 420): the hotbar, bars and status fill the lower right, so the pill climbs the right half to 0.2 of
  the height instead of falling back onto the slots.
- **Cards on screens under ~320 px tall** (only the wheel sweep's synthetic 750 x 311 / 640 x 280 / 600 x 250 / 560 x 220): the band is shorter than the readable minimum
  stack (128 px). The rows shrink past that minimum, so the card never leaves its band. Every phone and tablet keeps the minimum.
- **The friend chip** sits right of the closed MENU button. MENU has no free corner for it (its "!" uses the top-right).
- **Tests** that changed on purpose: R153's speed-popup test counts a moved-up MENU as an info row, because the popups are a world BillboardGui drawn under it. The
  inventory test keeps the Bag clear of MENU's old spot on a portrait phone. The beginner tutorial card keeps clear of the name rows (`BeginnerGuide.HotbarDetail`).
- **R155's byte-for-byte comparison with R154** (`run_camera155.sh` step 2): `perf155_opts.luau` now also allows the fitted card's layout. That covers the Position
  and Size of the card's pieces, plus the hint's anchor and text alignment now that it is centred under the name. It also covers where the collected seed starts its flight home, since it leaves from the fitted card. This is exactly the approved fit and hint move.
  Text, colours, transparency, timing and sounds are still compared. The rows published for the lucky tag live on a Folder under the card, which is never drawn.
- **Preview renderer**: two gaps in the R156 render are fixed in the scratch copies only (CanvasGroup fade, and text-less label strokes). Without the first fix the
  hidden bars' clover showed under the card.
