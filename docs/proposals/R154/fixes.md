Picture: `docs/proposals/R154/badges_popups.png` (before / after: the real ChestIndex, NotifyBadge151, SpeedPopupStyle and SpeedGainPopup of the R153 release and of this checkout on the Roblox mock, drawn with Pillow, DejaVu for Fredoka, plus the owner's two R153 screenshots; an approximation, not a Studio screenshot). Tests: `sh docs/proposals/R154/tests/run_fixes.sh <scratch dir> [mutate]` (in `tools/tests/run_all_suites.sh` as `R154run_fixes`). `Config.Version` is unchanged; no server rule, odds, saved data, remote or manifest entry changed; BackgroundMusic untouched; line 1 of every client script is still the R152 load guard.

## 1. The text in the notification bubble ("the number and signs in the notification bubble is also too small")

Cause: the count was `TextScaled` inside a `UITextSizeConstraint` (max 62% of the diameter), so the engine's own fit picked the size, and on the owner's screenshot it came out far below that maximum: the digits are about 17% of the bubble tall (7 px in a 45 px bubble), not the 40% the code intended. A fixed size cannot be shrunk by a fit.
* `NotifyBadge151.TextSize(size, text)`: **78% of the badge for one character** ("2", "!"; INDEX count 36 px -> text 28, MENU alert / DAILY 30 px -> 23) and **68% for two** ("9+": 24 / 20). `TextScaled` is off, the size constraint is gone, `B.Set` resizes when the text changes, `style` when the badge size does. White FredokaOne, the dark red edge, the centring on the disc, the ring, shine, pop, halo, sizes, overhangs and the wheel's 16 px margin are as in R153; a dot has no text.
* With FredokaOne's real metrics (measured on `shop_R120/tests/FredokaOne.ttf`: digits .745 em tall, "9+" 1.087 em wide, a line box centred on the digits): the digits are **58% of the badge / 70% of the red disc** for one character, **50% / 60%** for "9+" (60% of the disc is what fits: "9+" is as wide as the disc at 68%, so it cannot be as tall as a single digit inside a circle). The text stays inside the disc, centred, on 5 screens (computer and phones).
* Tests (R151 `test_indexbadge`, `test_indexbadge_daily`, `layout_badges.luau L.textFill`): text size >= 60% of the badge, digits >= 55% (<= 72%) of the disc, >= 45% of the badge, width between the ring's inner edges, corner inside the badge, centred, explicit size (no TextScaled / constraint), "9+" smaller than one character but >= 60%, nothing clips the badge (INDEX count, MENU alert, DAILY "3" and "9+", 1920 x 1080 .. 360 x 640). 3 new mutants (text small, "9+" too wide, TextScaled again); all 20 badge mutants are caught.
* Not measurable here: the engine's own TextScaled behaviour (the cause is read off the screenshot, not reproduced). The fix does not depend on it: the size is explicit.

## 2. Speed popups

### 20% smaller
`SpeedPopupStyle.Size`: text 44 -> **35**, bolt 40 -> **32**, box 300 x 72 -> **240 x 58**, bolt box 48 -> 38, gap 4 -> 3, outline 5 -> **4** (0.8 of R153, 1.6x R151, to whole pixels). The fan scales with them (`Fan.Base` 2 -> 1.6, `Max` 3 -> 2.4, `Min` .75 -> .6, the half popup 111 x 30 -> 89 x 24) so the arrangement and the pile-up stay what they were in R151 (46% of a popup under others on a computer and on most phones; R153 had up to 1.9x that on narrow phones: now at most 1.58x on a 320 x 568). The pooled field is 768 x 608 (was 960 x 760). The pop, fling, fade, life, 10 a second, colours, font and bolt are untouched.

### What made them glitchy (measured on the real client, frame by frame)
1. **The low-tier caps cut every popup short.** The stream is 10 popups a second and a popup lives 0.65 s, so 6-7 are alive at once. The own caps were 8 / 6 / 4 (tiers 3 / 2 / 1; tier 1 is FastMode and a slow frame rate, and `ClientFxBudget` steps down a tier for every 2.5 s under 42 fps, which Studio does; phones start on tier 2). At cap 4 every popup was cut at about 0.4 s and faded out in 0.08 s while still flying and opaque (55 of 60 popups at tier 1 on the R153 code, at 30, 60 and 144 fps): the half-faded popup in the owner's screenshot, the ones that are gone early, and the visible size mix (4 live + 1 fading + the newborn). **Own popups now hold 8 on every tier** (the cap is a burst guard, not a limit on the normal stream); other players keep 3 / 2 / 1.
2. **A busy pool reused a LIVE popup.** When every pooled frame was busy (the cap's live popups + the retired ones still fading: a lag spike delivers several awards at once) the oldest showing popup was taken for the new one and jumped to its place and text (12 times in 8 s with 0.35 s hitches on tier 3). Now the pool grows by one frame (`Style.Field.MaxFrames` = 24) and a showing frame is never touched; a burst drops no award.
3. **The pop-in**: every popup is born at 0.45 and overshoots to 1.07 before settling at 1.0 at 0.28 s. At 10 a second there is nearly always one newborn on screen at 45-90%: that is the "different sizes". It is the R151 pop the owner approved and the brief exception to "one size"; the numbers are `Style.Pop` (From .45, Time .28) if he wants it softer.
* Checked and **not** causes: per-frame position snapping (positions come from the popup's age on the clock, written when they move by more than 0.05 px; the flight decelerates monotonically at 30 / 60 / 144 fps), z-order flicker (every popup gets a new, higher `ZIndex` at birth; none changes during a life; no two showing popups share one), the fan clamping to the screen edge (it is chosen once per popup at its birth; a resize in flight changes no popup that is flying), transparency steps (opaque to 0.5 s, then a linear 0.15 s fade; no step is bigger than the fade's), the Head-anchored fan (already on the HumanoidRootPart at head height since R153: a head bob of +-0.4 stud every frame moves the fan by 0).

### Constant size
A popup is exactly the screen's unit x 1.0 (35 px text at 1080 p) from 0.30 s on whatever it is doing; before that only the pop-in (0.45 .. 1.07). The field is a BillboardGui sized in offsets (pixels): no scale component anywhere, so the camera distance never reaches it (identical at 6, 30 and 95 studs, frame by frame); the unit and fan are taken once per popup at its birth.

### Tests
`test_popups154.luau` (105 checks; the REAL client; 30 / 60 / 144 fps, tiers 1-3, FastMode, 0.35 s lag spikes + a 10-tick burst, a resize in flight, a head bob, cameras at 6 / 30 / 95 studs, Reduced Motion, a phone fan): sizes; constant size over the life (scale, text, bolt, box, outline, tilt); no showing frame reused; nothing cut short in the normal stream; nothing cut opaque, nothing dropped, the pool bounded, in a burst; every award shown; z order; frame-to-frame motion is a straight line, never backwards, never faster than its curve, decelerating; fade steps; the fan on the root. It fails on the R153 code (tier 1: 56 of 60 popups cut short; tier 3 with lag spikes: 12 live popups reused). `mutate154.py`: 18 breakages (caps back to 4 / 6, live reuse, no pool growth, shrink / grow in flight, size in studs, size by distance, unit per frame, head anchor, flat z, 24 px position steps, half-step alpha, opaque cut, R153 sizes, 5 px outline, a tiny pop, TextScaled), 17 caught by the new test, the 18th (tier 2 cap 6) by the R151 style test.
