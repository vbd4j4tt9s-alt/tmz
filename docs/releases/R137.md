## What changed
### Big packs (your approved proposal, `docs/proposals/pity_R136`)
- **New size table:** 5x+ goes from 1/167 to **1/71**, 10x+ from 1/2,000 to **1/775**, 25x from 1/100,000 to 1/25,000. It covers track, Mech, treadmill bonus and event packs.
- **Hidden pity for players** (you said no indicator and it has to feel random; nothing is shown anywhere):
  - Every pack you earn counts: steals, event packs and treadmill bonus packs. Bought Mech packs and gifts don't count.
  - After 18 small packs in a row, each new small pack has a growing chance to come out big. By the 30th it's always 5x+.
  - The 10x+ pity works the same way: it starts at 150 packs and is sure by 200.
  - A pity pack is just a normal pack of a bigger size, sometimes 7.5x, 10x or more.
  - It's saved with the profile.
  - Measured: a 5x+ about every 20 packs, never more than 30; a 10x+ about every 120, never more than 200.
- **Giant drops on the track** (also hidden, random spot, random timing):
  - The chance of a forced big pack grows with every refresh that had none. You get a 5x+ at least every 6 refreshes (30 min) and a 10x+ at least every 12 (1 h).
  - Measured: about 7 big packs per server-hour, and a 10x+ every ~36 min (was ~5 h).
  - They look and announce exactly like natural packs.

### Rarer Legendary and Mythic seeds
- **Rarer down the biome list.** Legendary and Mythic chances are divided by the biome's factor: Forest ×2, Jungle ×2.5, Desert ×3, Snow ×3.5, Lava ×4, Crystal ×5, Storm ×6. Epic, Legendary and Mythic packs get half of that.
  - Common pack Legendary: Forest 1/30 → **1/60** … Storm → **1/180**.
  - Common pack Mythic: Forest 1/200 → **1/400** … Storm → **1/1,200**.
- **No more pass-up.**
  - Desert's Mythic goes from 1/6 to **1/600**, and Crystal's from 1/26 to **1/1,000**.
  - Desert's Epic, Legendary and Mythic packs no longer always give the Mythic: 1/20, 1/8 and 1/5.
- **Packs people already have keep their old odds:** track, Bags and gardens. New packs carry odds version 137; old ones stay on 112.
- The Index shows the new chances.

### Leaderboard scrolls now
- **Why it didn't:** the board is in the world, where a list only scrolls by its very thin bar, and the mouse wheel zooms the camera instead.
- **Now:**
  - **▲ / ▼** page buttons with a scroll thumb;
  - the **mouse wheel** scrolls the board while you point at it (the camera doesn't zoom then);
  - you can **click-drag or finger-drag** the list;
  - FIND ME still works.

### No more low-detail pictures (hotbar, Bag, bonus reel, Index)
- **Hotbar, Bag and bonus reel:**
  - The flat 2D "icon" versions are **removed from the game**. Every picture on screen is always the real 3D pack, seed or fruit: with Low graphics too, and with any number on screen (the old limit was 60).
  - A picture that's still loading stays empty for a moment instead of showing the flat icon.
  - The bonus reel builds all its card pictures before it spins.
- **Index cards:** they no longer swap the full plant for the far-away low-detail plant.
- **Performance:**
  - The flat icons were extra GUI objects, built every time a picture was loading or scrolled away. They're gone now.
  - Pictures for things off screen are still released and reused.
  - Up to ~8 ms per frame goes to building pictures, and only while something on screen is waiting for one.

### Index polish
Preview: `docs/proposals/R137/index_compare.png`.
- **Each card has:**
  - a rarity chip and a 1/N chance chip;
  - a soft rarity glow and pedestal behind a bigger model, with the name under it;
  - **SEED ✓ / GROWN ✓** chips;
  - a gold **CLAIM** pill when there's a reward, or "★ COMPLETE" when it's fully found.
- **The rest of the screen:**
  - cards are sorted from Common to King;
  - the open biome tab has a white ring;
  - the header shows **FOUND x / y** across all biomes.
