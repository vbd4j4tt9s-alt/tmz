# R126: Mech packs and bonus rolls get a pack size

Needs R125 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R126_install.lua` (~24 KB, 5 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R126] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R126_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

Install order if none are in yet: R124, then R125, then R126.

## What changed

- **Bought Mech packs** now roll a pack size, using the same table as world packs. Each pack in a 5 or 10 bundle rolls on its own.
- **Treadmill bonus rolls** now roll a pack size too. That includes the Void Pack.
- Odds per pack:

  | 0.5x | 1x | 1.5x | 2.5x | 3.5x | 5x | 7.5x | 10x | 15x | 20x | 25x |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 3% | 80.8% | 13% | 2.8% | 0.35% | 0.04% | 0.008% | 0.002% | 0.0007% | 0.0002% | 0.0001% |

- A bigger pack gives a bigger seed, just like a world pack.
  - Mech seeds used to be locked at 1x. They now follow the pack size; a 1x Mech pack is exactly the same as before.
  - Note: 3% of bought Mech packs come out 0.5x, as world packs do.
- No new animation or screen. The roll window, the shop and the messages look exactly as before. The pack in the bag is simply bigger or smaller.
- Boot luck still has no effect on bonus rolls. Packs already in bags keep their size.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R126'`.

## Test in Studio

- **Mech:** buy a Mech 10-pack with Gems, then look at the pack sizes in your bag. Open a bigger one: the seed should be bigger too.
