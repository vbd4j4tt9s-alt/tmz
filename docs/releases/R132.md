# R132: polished market, Fruit of the Hour, home marker

Needs R131 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R132_install.lua` (~59 KB, 12 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R132] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R132_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed

**Market polish** (your current market, not a new building)
- The R69 building is unchanged. Added: string lights under the striped awning, three warm lamps over the counter (they light it at night), flower boxes under the side windows and produce stands beside the porch.
- The coloured balls and jar blocks are gone. In their place are the game's own models:
  - fruit on the counter, the produce stands and the sign;
  - plants in the planters and flower boxes;
  - seed packs on the shelves.
- Fruit models are used exactly as they are in the game (the watermelon is the big green cube).
- Each model is built on its own after the market. If one can't be built, it is skipped with a warning and the market still appears.
- No avatar, and no chalkboard sign.

**Fruit of the Hour**
- Every hour, one fruit sells for **×1.5 to ×3.0** more (in 0.1 steps).
  - It comes from all 51 obtainable fruits, never the same fruit two hours in a row.
  - The pick follows the server clock, so every server and every player sees the same fruit, with no messages needed.
- **Pedestal** to the right of the arrival spot as you face the market. It replaces the old sign, and has no discs or rings:
  - stone plinth, teal column, gold cradle, and a "FRUIT OF THE HOUR" plaque;
  - the real fruit floats above it, turning slowly;
  - a label above shows the fruit's name, "SELLS ×2.3" and the time left.
- **Selling:** selling that fruit (one, or Sell All) pays the bonus. In the sell menu its price includes the bonus, with a line "🌟 Fruit of the Hour ×2.3".
- **Notices:** everyone gets a gold notice when the hour changes. A player who joins is told the current fruit after 8 s.
- **Owner test:** `/test fruithour ember pumpkin 3` makes that fruit the Fruit of the Hour for 10 minutes.
  - The bonus can be 1.5–3; leave it out for ×3.
  - `fruithour` alone picks a fruit at random.
  - `fruithour off` goes back to the clock.

**Home marker**
- A 🏠 floats about 10 studs over the middle of your own base, above the centre path and clear of the beds. Only you see it.
- It is sized in studs, so it shrinks with distance, with a small minimum so you can still find it from the track. It bobs gently.
- If you move to another base, it moves with you.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R132'`.

## Test in Studio
- **Market:** walk around the market. You should see the lights, lamps, flower boxes, produce stands, and the game's fruit and plants on the counter, stands and planters.
- **Fruit of the Hour:**
  - Look at the pedestal: the fruit turns over it, and the label shows the name, bonus and time left.
  - `/test fruithour apple 3`: everyone gets the gold notice and the pedestal switches to the apple.
  - Harvest an apple and open the sell menu. It shows ×3.0 and sells for 3× its normal price.
  - `/test fruithour off` puts the hour's fruit back.
- **Home:** go to your base and look up at the 🏠 over the middle. With 2 players (Clients and Servers), each player sees it over their own base only.
