## What changed
The seed designs you approved (previews: `docs/proposals/seeds_R133/`):

- **Every seed looks different.** Seeds that were the same design in another colour now have their own shape and details. For example:
  - Storm Sovereign is a thunderbolt;
  - Silent Frostbell is a bell;
  - Polar Starbloom is a snowflake star;
  - Moon Melon is a crescent.
- **No "winged" side shards on any seed.** Five seeds are redesigned:
  - **Diamond Vine:** a vine wound round the seed with diamond leaves on it, and a diamond bud on top.
  - **Prism Pepper:** a bell pepper with three lobes, a green-crystal calyx and stem, and a rainbow glint.
  - **Ash Tomato:** sepals, a stem and glowing ember cracks.
  - **Iceberry:** an ice-spike calyx, frost swirls and an icicle.
  - **Venom Vine:** a dark vine coiled round it, with a glowing venom drop.
- **Prism Monarch:** the round wings are gone. 8 small crystals in pink, blue, mint, gold, violet and orange, all different sizes, grow from it. The crown fits the gem.
- **Nothing floats.** Every piece sits on the seed's surface, crowns included. The automatic check finds 0 of 45 seeds with a floating part; before, 15 seeds had some.
- **Effects grow with rarity.** These show on held and dropped seeds:
  - Rare: a soft glow and rising motes.
  - Legendary: + an orbit, and the seed's own parts animate (flames flicker, crystals twinkle, glows pulse).
  - Mythic: + a second orbit with trails.
  - Secret: + an eclipse ring.
  - Cosmic: + a bigger double orbit.
  - King: + a floating crown and slow golden rays.
- The animations only change colour, so a seed in your hand never moves out of place. Far-away seeds skip them.
- Mech seeds are unchanged.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R134'`.
