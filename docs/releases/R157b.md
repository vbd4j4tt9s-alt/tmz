Needs **R157 installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R157'`). **One installer:** `installers/R157b_install.lua` (~111 KB, 25 scripts,
nothing moved or deleted). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R157b] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R157b'` after it.

## What changed
### Bugs fixed
- **The HUD could die on join** (your Output: `AudioMixer is not a valid member of ReplicatedStorage` and then "Requested module experienced an error
  while loading" in Hotbar, Index, shop, DAILY ...). The title screen loaded the click sound before the game had finished loading. Now it waits for
  the game to load first, and the sound scripts wait for what they need instead of crashing. A click on the title before the game has loaded has no
  click sound; after that it sounds as before. The music script waits the same way, so a session can't start without music.
- **Track music switched to base music in the Desert.** Your place streams (Roblox loads far parts in and out). Far down the track your game unloaded
  the line that marks where the track starts, so the music thought you had left the track. Now it uses a number that never unloads.

### Changes you asked for
- **MENU at A on PC:** the MENU button sits a third of the way down. On smaller PC windows the whole HUD is the same layout as your screenshot,
  just smaller; MENU never gets smaller than 85% of its full size, and the open wheel never touches your balances. Text keeps a readable size,
  BONUS ROLL stays 7 px over the pity bars, and menus keep a gap over the hotbar. **Phones and tablets: exactly as before** (a test locks them).
- **"THE TRACK" sign** on the gate is gone.
- **Mech packs with a full Bag:** both buy buttons say **Bag full**. A press shows "BAG FULL! MAKE ROOM FIRST" (or "MAKE ROOM FOR 10 PACKS FIRST"
  when you picked more than fit) and never opens a purchase. The buttons update by themselves when your Bag gets room. Bonus rolls already said
  BAG FULL. A Robux purchase already paid for is still always given.
- **DAILY:** no white tile behind the mystery pack (quests and login days); the pack keeps a thin light edge. The **TODAY** tag is white with a
  black outline.
- **Title tips** are calmer: a small, slow pulse (±3%, one breath every 1.8 s), no bob.
- **Quieter Output:** the two tree models that aren't yours and the item-picture loading note are plain notes now, not orange warnings.
