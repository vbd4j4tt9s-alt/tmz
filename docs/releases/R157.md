# R157: new pity bars, reveal fixes, DAILY + INVITE in the MENU wheel, a calmer Bag, title tips, "you" / "your" everywhere, the Void Pack after 20 minutes, the secret pyramid

Needs **R156 installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R156'`). **One installer:** `installers/R157_install.lua` (~236 KB, 53 scripts). Edit mode,
Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for **`[R157] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start
a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R157'` after it.

- It refuses if R156 isn't in ("Config is not the version this update was built for. Nothing changed."): paste R156 first.
- Nothing is retired this time (no module moved into the backup).
- `BackgroundMusic` is NOT changed this time.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R157_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## Before you publish
1. Shut Down All Servers after publishing (or Migrate To Latest Update). Old servers have the old pyramid and no 20-minute rule; nothing breaks
   if R156 and R157 servers run side by side (both new saved fields are kept as they are by an older server).
2. **The Void Pack 20-minute rule counts from now, for everyone.** Play time was never counted before, so every player starts at 0 the first time they
   join an R157 server, even someone who has played for weeks. Someone who already claimed keeps their pack and still sees "CLAIMED ✓".
3. **The pyramid** is built by the game's code from your model's numbers (no asset to load, no permission needed). The Sunscar Pyramid is gone from the
   Desert. Nothing else in the Desert moved.

Profile version stays 22 (two new optional saved fields: `Premium.PlaySeconds157`, `Premium.Secrets156`).

## What changed
### Pity bars (shade "Fresh")
- NORMAL is clover green, EVENT is gold with a clover-green rim. The clover icon replaces the diamonds.
- The bars sit right above the hotbar: 8 px on a PC, 6 px on a phone. The name of the item you hold now sits just above the bars (hidden on the
  smallest portrait phones, 360 x 740 and smaller).
- The event notice and tag use a gold star ⭐. The lucky pop uses the bar's own colours.

### Pack reveal
- **SKIP only for Secret, Cosmic and King.** Common..Mythic have no SKIP: click / tap / Enter / B still collect once the result shows.
- In story scenes the SKIP pill sits in the bottom-right corner (14 px in).
- The Common..Mythic card now fits between BASE / TRACK and the pity bars.
- "click to collect!" ("tap to collect!" on phones) sits right under the seed's name.

### MENU wheel
- **DAILY and INVITE are in the MENU wheel** now: SETTINGS, INDEX, SHOP, DAILY, INVITE in a half circle. BASE / TRACK have the top row to themselves.
- DAILY's badge sits on its button's corner, with nothing over it.
- While the wheel is closed, MENU shows a red **"!"** when a daily or Index reward is waiting.
- On phones the MENU button moves up a bit to make room.

### Bag (option 2)
- No green: the Bag is navy like the hotbar. Dragging over a slot shows a thin light rim instead of the lime flood. The Discard bin stays red.
- The hint line ("Click to hold • ...") is gone.

### Title screen tips (style B)
- One tip floats between the logo and "Click to play!", a new one every 10 s with a fade, a small pulse and a bob, some words in yellow or green.
- 24 tips in simple words: how to play ("walk on the treadmill to get faster", what the shovel and bat do, ...), plus "dont look into the pyramid" and
  "beware the darkened".
- To make room, the logo is 7% smaller on a landscape phone and a little smaller on 1366 x 768 / 1280 x 720 windows. The button never moves.

### "you" / "your" everywhere
- Every "u" / "ur" the player sees is now "you" / "your" / "you're" (138 texts in 32 scripts). Nothing else in the wording changed.

### Free Void Pack: after 20 minutes of play
- Under 20 minutes the pedestal says **play 12 more min to claim** (whole minutes) and Hold E is off. At 20 minutes it opens as before.
- Total play across visits, saved. Players the giveaway already owed a pack still get it.

### The secret pyramid (Desert)
- **Your Classic Pyramid replaces the Sunscar Pyramid**, same spot, scaled to 0.6834 so it fits the old footprint (44.8 x 44.8, 20.2 studs tall).
  Hollow, walk-through like every landmark.
- A plain **Desert Mythic pack** floats inside. **Hold E** from next to the pyramid (or inside). Each player gets it **once**: it counts when banked,
  then it's gone for that player only.
- The **Sand Snake** chases like for any stolen Desert pack. Caught, hit, fell in a hole or off the map, died or left: the pack goes straight back into the
  pyramid for that player (no drop on the track).
- A **biome refresh** never banks the secret pack either: it goes back into the pyramid ("BIOMES REFRESHING! THE PACK WENT BACK IN THE PYRAMID"), and Hold E
  is refused during a refresh and in the 15 seconds before it ("THE BIOMES REFRESH SOON! TRY AGAIN AFTER THE REFRESH").
- The 42 keyboard keys under the pyramid are left out (they poked through its first step).

`Config.Version = 'V150 R157'`.
