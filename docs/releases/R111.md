# R111: shorter tutorial with your avatar, straight arrows, Ember King eye

Needs R110 installed first (it patches the R110 scripts and refuses otherwise, changing nothing).
Installer: `installers/R111_install.lua`. Same steps as before: Edit mode, Play stopped, paste all of it into the
Command Bar, Enter, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R111_TutorialV2_Backup.Installer)("undo")`
(undo R111 before undoing R110).

## What changed (6 scripts)

| Script | Change |
|---|---|
| BeginnerGuide | 5 steps: steal, run home, open, plant, then 4 quick tips. No waiting for the plant to grow. Grade-3 words. "Steal A Pack". |
| BeginnerTutorial | Your avatar + name on the guide badge. Straight arrow line from your feet to the goal every frame (goes through walls; only you see it). Bigger arrows. Welcome at the top so you and the arrows stay visible. Tips with a NEXT button. Skip button shows "X". |
| TitleScreen | New players skip the title: it closes by itself once their save loads (after at least 1.2 s). Everyone else sees it as before. |
| TreeReworkMotion + TreeReworkData5 | Ember King: the dark lava crust on its glowing dome (the "pupil") darts to random spots, holds, darts again. |
| Config | `Config.Version = 'V149 R111'`. |

## Whose avatar

The game owner's (the group owner's if a group owns the game). In Studio it is your own account.
To force a specific account, set `G.GuideUserId = <user id>` at the top of `ReplicatedStorage.BeginnerGuide`.

## Test in Studio

To see the tutorial with an existing save: Settings -> Replay tutorial. To see the title skip, use a fresh save
(e.g. a second test account, or clear your data).

- [ ] New player: title closes quickly, welcome shows at the top with your avatar, arrows start under your feet.
- [ ] Arrows always start at your feet and point straight at the pack, even through walls; they never drift aside.
- [ ] Steal -> run home -> open (pointer on the hotbar pack) -> plant -> 4 tips -> "You're ready!".
- [ ] Ember King (grown): the dark crust on the yellow dome looks around.
