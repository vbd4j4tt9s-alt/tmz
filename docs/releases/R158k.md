# R158k: store fixes ("Not enough cash!", the money counter never shows more than you have, prices shown = prices charged)

Needs **R158j installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158j'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158k_install.lua` (~68 KB, 14 scripts,
one new module `StoreMoney158k`). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter,
wait for **`[R158k] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158k'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158k_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. No saved-data change.

## What was wrong
- **The money counter rounded UP.** $10,350,000 showed "$10.4M" next to a $10.4M item; $1.75T showed "$1.8T" next to $1.8T boots. Players
  thought they had enough, the server (correctly) said no. This is most likely what you saw.
- A button you couldn't afford turned grey and did nothing when pressed (no sound, no message).
- The market could use an old balance (from before a sale's money landed) and say "Need $X" while the HUD already showed enough.
- A Buy press could be dropped if the market was refreshing at that moment.
- "Not enough" was worded 5 different ways; while your save was still loading the market said "Need $..." because it read your cash as 0.

The server never charged twice and never let anything through without enough money.

## What changed
- The money counter (HUD and the market's Cash line) **rounds down**: it never shows more than you have.
- **Every Buy button shows its exact price and can be pressed.** Not enough money: a "denied" click and a plain red **"Not enough cash!"**
  (or **"Not enough gems!"**), nothing is sent. Bag full, already owned and max level keep their own messages.
- **Prices are always the same:** every button and sign shows exactly what the server charges, the same for every player and screen.
  Each press sends the price it showed; if it ever differs, nothing is charged and you see "The price changed! Check the new price."
- Having exactly the price is enough.

## Prices that change on purpose (left as they are; say if you want them fixed)
- Treadmill upgrade per level: $250K, $11.9M, $562M, $26.7B, $6.1T, $180T.
- Fence upgrade per level: $500K, $18M, $646M, $23.2B, $1.56T, $72T.
- Mech pack: 80 / 375 / 700 gems for 1 / 5 / 10, on sale until the limited event ends.
- Cash to Gems: $1T per gem.
