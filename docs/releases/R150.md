# R150: bonus roll with personality, a special mystery pack pedestal, every input has a sound (and every sound on the beat)

Needs **R149 installed** (both parts). Installer: `installers/R150_install.lua` (~237 KB, 59 scripts). One paste.
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter. **Wait for `[R150] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R150_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

**Publishing:** no saved-data change (profile version stays 22). Restart servers so everyone gets the new code.

## What changed
### Bonus roll (button, gift timer, roll screen)
- **Button**: a chunky candy button with a drawn gift.
  - **Ready**: sunny gold; every few seconds the gift wiggles, the button bounces and a shine sweeps across; the count badge sits on the gift at 2 and pops when it changes; a new roll pops in with sparkles and a bright note.
  - **Charging (new)**: while you're on the treadmill and nothing is ready, the button shows in calm lilac and fills with gold as the next roll nears: "NEXT 3:12", then "ALMOST! 0:24". Tapping it says how long is left.
  - Refusals show short text on the button ("BAG FULL!") with a "not allowed" ping.
- **Gift timer** (over your head on the treadmill): a purple pill with a gift that fills with colour, a progress bar and the countdown; "ALMOST THERE!" in pink in the last 30 s; a pop, shake and sparkles with "BONUS READY! 🎁 OPEN IT!"; "2 ROLLS READY! TAP BONUS ROLL" at the limit.
- **Roll screen**: gift-wrap stripes and a bow on the header, bouncing gem pointers, rarity plaques on the cards, playful lines while it spins ("Ooh, what could it be?"), a reveal word that grows with rarity (NICE! … LEGENDARY! … SECRET!!!) with turning light rays, confetti (behind the header) and sparkles; COLLECT, or AWESOME! for Legendary and up.
- Timing, ticks, odds and results unchanged. The first tick of the first roll is no longer silent. A double tap on SKIP can't reveal twice. Reduced motion: no ambient motion, short spin.

### Mystery pack pedestal (every base)
- **A sculpted pedestal in your treadmill's biome colours**: paved apron, plinth with four gem posts, stepped base with gold trim, column with gold collars, and a glowing pad under the pack.
- **Locked**: calm violet glow, a dim ring of 8 gems orbiting the black pack, faint wisps, a gold padlock on the column.
- **Unlocked**: everything goes gold, the gem ring speeds up, sparkles rise, a soft light column over your own pedestal.
  - **The moment it unlocks** (once, your pedestal): the padlock pops open and drops, a ring, a flash, sparks, a chime, the pack hops, the sign pops.
- **Taking it**: the pack flies into you (like a picked fruit) and pops sparks, with the pickup sound on landing. Others see it fly.
- **Taken**: dim mint, no effects. The sign is a rounded pill (same size, place and distances as before).
- Gameplay, timings and odds unchanged. Pedestals far away (75+ studs) cost nothing.

### Sounds: every input has one, and they're on the beat (an Opus audit of all 50 sounds and every input)
- **In sync now**: the harvest sound plays when the fruit lands in your hotbar (not when it lifts off); every pack-opening click sounds; Common / Uncommon reveal pops on the beat; The Darkened's arrival sound joins at the right point if the message arrives late; big reveal booms never play late; the "track reopens" countdown changes with the beep; keeper slam and hit sounds never double up or go missing; Verity's greeting never starts late.
- **New sounds** (all existing game sounds, nothing uploaded): equipping a hotbar slot (keys 1–0, gamepad, tap); a low "not allowed" ping on every refusal (red notices, teleport / dig refusals, market errors, Verity, bonus roll, "make room in your Bag"); menus open and close with the same sound whether you use X, tap outside, Esc or B; selecting a plant; coins reaching your wallet; teleport arrival; KaChing on a bought upgrade; fruit gifts; the garden shovel; the title screen start; bat swings; other players' Secret / Cosmic / King pulls; a preview when you move a volume slider; holding E starts with a click.
- **Volume**: Effects at 0 now mutes everything (keeper snores and jump / land sounds too). Your saved volumes apply from the first second you join.
- The Index no longer chimes before a pack reveal (it gave away good pulls).
