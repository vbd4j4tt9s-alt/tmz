## What changed
### Pity: every 10th pack is lucky
- **Two counts per player, both saved.** **EVENT pity** = Void, Verity and Mech packs (one count between the three). **NORMAL pity** = every other pack.
- In each, the **10th open is LUCKY**: that roll gets **x1.5 luck on top of everything** (boots x clover; the clover alone on Void / Verity / Mech). Then
  the count goes back to 0. A lucky roll's cap is x1.5 too (up to x75M), so the x1.5 always counts.
- Unchanged: the per-tier limits inside a pack (King 1%, Cosmic 5%, Secret 25%, Mythic 45%), the 80% rule and every odds number shown.
- A failed or refused open (full bag, data loading ...) doesn't move the count. Owner / Studio TEST packs and opens with `/test boots` don't count.
- **Two bars, always on, just above the hotbar:** **gold** "3/10 pity" and **purple** "3/10 event pity". The bar of the pack u hold is highlighted;
  9/10 glows; the lucky pack pops with a shine and "LUCKY PACK" on the reveal. Fits phones (checked on 36 screen sizes); Reduced Motion turns the
  movement off.
- The rule is listed with the odds: the hold tooltip ("LUCKY PACK: x1.5 luck on this one!"), the Mech shop cards and `/test odds`.

### Inventory: works like Roblox's own (keeps our look)
- **Number keys 1-9 / 0** equip or put away; click / tap equips, again puts away. The slot lights up on the very press.
- **The Bag** (Bag button, the ` key or B; gamepad DPad-Up) shows only what's **not** on the hotbar, with search, the filters and a **143/200** count
  (amber from 180, red **FULL** at 200).
- **Move items every way:** drag slot <-> slot (swaps), slot -> Bag (the slot stays **blank**), Bag -> slot. On a phone hold 0.4 s, then drag, or
  **tap-tap**: hold and let go to pick an item, then tap where it goes. Right-click or gamepad Y picks too.
- **A blank hotbar is possible:** put everything in the Bag and it stays blank; new items go to the Bag. Nothing ever jumps out of the Bag by itself.
- **New items:** join their stack, else the first free slot, else the Bag. Never pushes anything off the hotbar.
- **Ur layout is saved** across respawns and rejoins.
- **Item info panel:** hover a slot or Bag card (PC), pick it (phone), or select it (gamepad): the pack's odds, the pity line, the Mech coat line.
- **200 items max** (packs, seeds, fruit; a stack of 10 counts 10; garden plants and gear don't count). Every way of getting an item says "make room"
  instead (the item waits: bonus roll stays ready, daily claim stays open, gifts stay in the inbox, fruit stays on the plant). A pack u carried home
  always banks. A Robux Mech purchase is never lost: the shop only offers what fits, and a receipt that arrives anyway is granted. Players already
  above 200 keep everything but get nothing new until they're under.
- **Discard:** drop an item on the **🗑 Discard** bin in the Bag (or pick it and tap the bin). The popup asks "throw it away? u sure? it's gone forever",
  lets u choose how many from a stack (1, a number or All) and needs a **~1 s hold** on "hold to discard" for **every** item. Not while u carry a stolen
  pack or are in a chase. Checked by the server; every discard is logged in the Output and `/test hotbar`.

### Pack openings
- **Cinematic camera for Secret / Cosmic / King:** the camera cuts between shots inside the story scene (the designs u approved). No opening move into
  the scene and no hand-back move after (the push-in and cut stay as they were). **Background blur on every device.** The Cosmic seed shows with the star
  (~0.5 s later). Common to Mythic look the same as in R154.
- **Clicking never skips any more** (no spam-skip, on any pack). The only skip is the small **SKIP ▸▸** button at the bottom right (gamepad B / R2,
  Enter on a keyboard). Collecting the waiting seed is still a click anywhere, but only a click that starts after the result shows.

### Mech packs
- **Every Mech pack u buy** (gems or Robux) rolls **Gold 4.5% / Diamond 0.5%**, like world packs. The seed, plant and fruit keep the coat (a coated fruit
  pays x3 / x6). A Gold / Diamond Mech pack keeps its cyan lights, MECH letters and hazard stripes. The coat line is on the shop card and the tooltip,
  and the "Bought" notice names any coats.
- The card's "LIMITED TIME!" now counts down to **1 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC** (the Verity event's end), and then the pack goes off sale.

### Luck
- **Boots halved:** Sand x25, Frost x250, Lava x10K, Crystal x500K, Thunder x25M (cap 25M; with the clover x50M). The clover is still x2.

### UI and lag
- **Speed popups follow the zoom:** exactly R154 at the default camera distance, smaller as u zoom out, gone past ~36 studs, never bigger than 1.3x close
  up.
- **Ur clover picture** on the HUD luck row, shop card and purchase pop.
- **DAILY / INDEX / notification bubbles** a bit smaller and moved so they don't cover the button pictures.
- **B3 on graphics tier 1 too:** the keyboard track's near letters at 12 px a stud on tiers 1 and 2 (PC tier 3 unchanged).
