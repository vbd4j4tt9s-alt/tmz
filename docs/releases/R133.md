- **Soil:** the sunflower and pineapple planters and all 4 window flower boxes now have soil, and the plants grow out of it.
- **Front:** there is a real timber wall behind the front MARKET sign, from the sign up to the roof beam. You can't see through to the inside any more.
- **No MARKET signs inside.** The door signs only show "MARKET" on their outside face, and the front sign only on its front.
- **Lights hang from things now.**
  - The 3 lamps hang from a new ceiling beam that rests on the two side door frames.
  - The string lights hang on a wire tied to both porch posts, with every bulb on the wire.
  - The two porch lanterns hang from the porch beam.
- **Back of the market:** 4 pots with the game's plants (blueberry, lantern fern, tiger orchid, sunflower), a water barrel, and a stack of crates.
- **Nothing floating or disconnected.**
  - The produce stands are solid steps on the ground (the middle step used to hover).
  - The counter crates sit on the counter (they hovered 0.05 studs above it).
  - The porch posts got stone footings down to the grass.
  - An automatic check over every part of the market finds 0 floating pieces (the R131 market had 15).

**Fruit of the Hour**
