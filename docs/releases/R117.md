## Looks and effects (all effects client-side, budget-gated)

- **Treadmills:** every tier gets a numbered plaque (I-VII) and console pips, plus its own decorations and effects:
  lanterns/fireflies, braziers, glowing sun crest, icicles and aurora ribbon, lava channels with orbiting molten rocks,
  rainbow-cycling Prism with orbiting shards, and Thunder Runner with tesla coils, a storm cloud, a lightning arch and arcs.
  Training start plays a themed burst. Belt, prompt and training are unchanged.
- **Trails:** richer multi-layer ribbons (glow and reach grow per tier) and per-tier effects: Mint sparkles, Arc
  electric streaks, Solar embers, Aurora veil, Nebula star dust + spiral ribbon, Royal gold glitter, twisting gold/purple
  ribbons, a crown of sparkles and footstep glints. Shop previews show the new looks.
- **Boots:** more detail per tier (Sand 22 -> Thunder 38 parts per leg; gems, glowing seams, prisms, tesla coils) and
  more effects: frost mist, magma splashes, rainbow prisms, and for Thunder a lightning strike on landing / sprint start,
  arcs between the boots and an electric field when idle.
- **Budgets:** low quality / FastMode cuts effects to your own (or off for treadmills); only nearby on-screen players get
  full effects; Reduced Motion stops motion, bursts and strikes.
- `Config.Version = 'V149 R117'`.
