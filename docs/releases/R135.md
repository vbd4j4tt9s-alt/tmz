# R135: market fruit that stands

Needs R134 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R135_install.lua` (~24 KB, 2 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R135] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R135_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed
The market fruit you approved (preview: `docs/proposals/market_R135/market_fruit_compare.png`):

- **No more berries.** Blueberry and Iceberry fruit are three balls that only touch at one point each, so they always looked like they were floating. They're gone from the market. Only solid fruit is used now:

  | Spot | Before | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | Counter crates | apple, iceberry, ash tomato | prickly pear, pineapple, lantern (it glows) |
  | Right stand | watermelon, apple, blueberry | watermelon, apple, grape bunch |
  | Left stand | pumpkin, ash tomato, iceberry | pumpkin, ash tomato, snow melon |

- **Fruit sits instead of balancing.** Some fruit has a little stub or point underneath, like the lantern and the pineapple. That part now sinks into the crate or step, and the fruit rests on its body.
- Plants still grow straight out of the soil, and packs stand on their bottom.
- **The inside shelves stay full.** All 4 shelves hold 5 packs each (20 packs), a random mix of biomes and tiers on every server with no repeats. This has been in since R133.
- Nothing in the market floats: the automatic check over every part finds 0 floating pieces.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R135'`.
