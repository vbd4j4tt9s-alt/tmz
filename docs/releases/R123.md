Needs R121 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R123_install.lua` (~320 KB, 68 scripts).

Test commands for everything: `docs/COMMANDS.md` (also `/test help` or F4 in game).

## What changed

**Shop and giving**
- The DOUBLE SPEED banner is gone.
- Prices read "49 Robux" with no icon.
- There are no gift buttons.
- Seed packs and seeds can be given to a nearby player, the same way as fruit.

**Garden**
- Runners no longer get stuck on the small garden bed edges.

**Rarity**
- Emblems are removed everywhere.
- Each rarity has its own card border:
  - Common, Uncommon and Rare have still borders.
  - Legendary has a shine sweep.
  - Mythic has a glowing pulse.
  - Secret has a silver shimmer.
  - Cosmic has sparkles.
  - King has gem corners.

**Refresh**
- The refresh wall and the black cover are sized to the track.
- The wall uses the HUD moon picture.

**The Darkened (was the Veiled One)**
- It guards 2 Void Packs. They are space-themed, and the halo is kept.
- It stays through refreshes and leaves after the last pack is stolen and banked.
- Only one event can run at a time.
- After 3 refreshes with no steal, the packs reroll.
- Its arrival plays sound 113339179211972 and a 1-second lights-out, with a small circle of light around each player.
- The bottom card shows "ARRIVES IN 3m 12s" between visits.

**Keepers**
- Flings go higher for each biome's keeper, and players always land inside the track walls.
- The ragdoll lasts 2.7–3.3 s.
- Every keeper has its own hit animation. When a hit lands on the server is unchanged.

**Shovel holes on the track**
- Players can dig up to 4 holes each. Each hole is 1 player wide and lasts 3 minutes.
- Only a player carrying a pack falls in: they ragdoll for 4 s and drop the pack.
- A 5th hole is allowed only after one of your holes traps someone, you cover one, one expires, the track refreshes, or you leave.
- Holes can't be dug on pack spots, keeper homes, The Darkened or its Void Packs, or the entrance.
- The dig sound is chopped into variants. Run the DigSoundAnalyzer once to set the cuts.

**Treadmill bonus rolls**
- Every 10 minutes on the treadmill a roll becomes ready, and a gold button appears.
- Up to 2 rolls can wait. Time past that is lost, and leaving the game loses unclaimed rolls.
- Progress toward the next roll is saved, and a bar above the player on the treadmill shows the time left.
- The roll is a sliding strip of pack cards with a click per card. The server picks the result.
- Odds:

  | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 38% | 25% | 15% | 7% | 10% | 5% |

  These are the same as the packs that spawn on the track. Luck doesn't change them.
- The pool depends on the best treadmill owned:
  - Forest → Forest packs;
  - each treadmill adds its biome;
  - Storm → every biome.
  - The Void Pack and Mech packs are never in the pool.
- Legendary and Mythic results get a flash and a fanfare.

**Sound and effects**
- 8 sound timing fixes, including:
  - the countdown number now changes on its beep;
  - the chase alarm and the first notice sounds no longer play late;
  - the bag-tear sound now plays on slower connections;
  - keeper hit sounds arriving more than 0.75 s late are skipped.
- Seed sparkles, storm sparks and hole dirt are smoother and lighter on low graphics.

**Fixes**
- Holes near the Void Packs are no longer allowed.
- Falling in a hole shows one message instead of two.
- Gift inboxes are checked every 60 s instead of 15–30 s, to stay inside Roblox's save-data limits.
  Gifts still arrive right away; only the background tidy-up is slower.

**Owner commands**
- New commands: `fling`, `ragdoll`, `holes`, `dig`, `bonus`, `gifts`, `admins`.
- The help list is grouped by area. Duplicate rows and the outdated `balance84` command are removed.
