## What changed
### Keyboard track (like your reference video)
- **The whole track is keys now**, edge to edge (60 keys across, about 3 studs each), from the start to The Darkened.
- **Each biome is one candy colour in four shades:** Forest mint, Jungle pistachio, Desert caramel, Snow vanilla / ice blue, Lava strawberry, Crystal pink-purple, Storm chocolate.
- **Letters read the right way up and in keyboard order** as you run forward (Q W E R T Y left to right).
- **Every biome starts with a big cream spacebar** with its name (DESERT …), readable as you arrive.
- Keys near you press down and click under players and keepers. Further away they turn into simpler blocks that still look like keys: no flat colour bands, no bare floor even at top speed.
- Shovel holes show on top of the keys, near and far.

### Keeper speed signs
- **About a third of the size** (it never grows on big keepers) and gone beyond 160 studs.
- **Always a number, rounded up, never below what you really need:** under a million it's whole (`0`, `510`, `3,800`, `18K`, `540K`); from a million up one decimal is allowed (`1.2M`, `2.9M`, `16M`, `2.6B`, `22B`). The Darkened shows `∞`.

### Friend boost = faster speed gain
- Each Roblox friend in your server makes the treadmill give **+10% speed gain** (up to 3 friends, +30%). **Walk speed is no longer boosted.**
- Bought speed, gifts and bonus rolls aren't boosted. The friend notices and the invite message say "speed gain".

### Verity
- **She's a big yellow ball (22 studs) with her smiley face**, on a stage **south of the market**, past the fast-travel point. She turns to look at you and bobs.
- **Her voice:** she says her greeting (sound `123997993114202`) when you Talk to her, and once when you first walk up (at most once a minute). She swells a little while talking.
- **The "!" over her goes away once you've talked to her**, until you leave and rejoin. Her sign is one stack (marker, VERITY, timer) that never overlaps.
- **Her window** shows her face, the Void pack → Verity pack pictures, and **"EVENT ENDS IN 27d 04h 12m 09s"**.
- **The event ends 1 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC.** After that she refuses Void packs ("THE VERITY EVENT HAS ENDED"). To change the date: `ReplicatedStorage.LimitedEvent.EndsAt` (UTC seconds).

### Verity pack, seed and fruit
- **Verity pack:** our normal pack, painted yellow, with Verity's smiley on the front and back.
- **Verity seed:** a small yellow ball with her face; it grows into a giant one on its leaves, and the fruit has her face too.

### Index
- **MECH and VERITY are one tab: LIMITED**, with a new gem-and-star icon and a countdown ("ENDS IN 27d …", "LIMITED 27d" on the tab).
- Each set keeps its own rewards (MECH SET and VERITY rows with +10 / +100 gems); nothing already claimed changes.
- **Odds and amounts have no decimals under a million:** `1/6.3` → `1/6`, `1/10,000` → `1/10K`, `$240K`. Millions and up keep one decimal (`1/1.67B`, `$1.5M`). Same in the LIMITED store and the hold tooltips.

### Shop
- The "Complete the Roblox purchase prompt." strip is gone.
- **A successful purchase** (Robux, game pass or gems) shows "✅ Purchased: 10 Mech Packs!", plays the gem chime and pops confetti + sparkles. Once per purchase; never on a cancel.

### Mystery pack pedestal
- The sign sits **above** the pack (the bar never covers it) and is sized like a real sign, so it shrinks with distance and hides past 90 studs (120 for your own).

### New and changed seeds (full detail: `docs/releases/R148_seed_roster.md`)
| Seed | Change | Grow / regrow | Per fruit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aloe** (new, Desert **Rare**) | blue-green rosette, 3 spikes full of red-to-yellow flowers | 16 / 9 min | 10M (×3) |
| **Sand Fruit** (new, Desert **Legendary**) | a round barrel cactus (made from the game's own cactus pieces) with 4 lumpy sand-texture fruits | 46 / 25 min | 26.4M (×4) |
| **Fire Pepper** (Lava) | Rare → **Mythic**, your existing pepper model at **twice the size** | 72 / 40 min | 285M (×4), was 26.9M |
| **Moon Melon** (Crystal) | Rare → **Legendary** | 57 / 31 min | 509M (×2), was 168M |

- Every biome now has a Rare, a Legendary and a Mythic (Desert was missing Rare and Legendary, Lava a Mythic, Crystal a Legendary).
- **Growth variations:** the Aloe and the Sand Fruit each come in **4 different shapes**, like the other plants (a crop's own id decides which, so it never changes): Aloes differ in leaf count and length, spike heights, lean and places, flower density and colour (red-heavy, golden ...), spot pattern; Sand Fruits in barrel height and width, rib count (7-9), spine density, pups (0-3) and where and how big the 4 fruits are. A small size / turn variation comes on top. Fire Pepper keeps its one shape (x2) with its small size / turn variations.
- **Fire Peppers and Moon Melons players already have earn the new values** (Bag seeds, planted crops, even fruit already ripe on the plant). Fruit already picked keeps its value.
- **Packs players already hold can't roll Fire Pepper or Moon Melon any more** (no free Mythics); their Rare tier-mates take the share and every other seed keeps exactly its old odds. New packs: Fire Pepper 1/800, Moon Melon 1/150 in a Common pack; Aloe 1/8, Sand Fruit 1/90 in a Common Desert pack. New Desert packs from Epic up no longer give the Prickly Pear.
- **Index:** Desert has 7 seeds now. A Desert halfway / completion you had already reached keeps counting (recorded the first time you join after R148); new players need all 7. Claimed rewards stay claimed.
- Pack tooltips list seeds from the commonest to the rarest. The old retired "Aloe" seed shows as "Aloe Sprout". The rarity glow goes to the rarest plants first.
