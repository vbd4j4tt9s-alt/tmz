# R120: Robux shop redesign (reference layout, phone layouts)

Needs R119 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R120_install.lua` (~96 KB).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R120_Backup.Installer)("undo")` (undo R120 before R119).

## What changed (6 scripts, 1 new)

- **One scrolling shop page** (no tabs) in the reference style: green glossy "Shop" header with a red X, dark studded
  body, sections FEATURED (limited Mech pack banner with outcome odds and 1/5/10 picker), -- PASSES -- (x2 Growth,
  x2 Speed), -- SPEED -- ("DOUBLE Your SPEED x1 ▶ x2" banner + 5 speed bundles), -- MONEY -- (5 cash bundles),
  -- GEMS -- (cash to gems).
- **Cards:** bright gradients with light rays, big outlined amounts, purple Gems price + green Robux price buttons;
  passes keep the gift button and show a gold OWNED when owned.
- **Quick-jump buttons** (Featured, Passes, Speed, Money, Gems) on the right scroll to their section and highlight it.
- **Phones:** panel stays clear of the Roblox top bar, thumbstick and jump button; 2 cards per row on narrow panels;
  in portrait the jump buttons become a row above the panel.
- Same products, prices, purchase prompts, gem buys and gifting as before. Links from other menus still open the right
  section. Closing the shop and reopening it starts at the top.
- `Config.Version = 'V149 R120'`.
