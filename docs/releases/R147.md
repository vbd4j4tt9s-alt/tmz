## What changed
### Mystery pack pedestal (every base)
- A pedestal in the empty corner of each base, across from the treadmill. On it: a **black silhouette pack** spinning slowly.
- **Play 15 minutes in a day and it unlocks.** The sign counts down (`🔒 12:41`), then the pack lights up: **UNLOCKED! TAKE IT 🎁**. Only the base owner can take it. After that it says ✓ TAKEN TODAY and counts down to the next one.
- Time counts across rejoins and servers. A new one comes every day at midnight UTC (same reset as 🎁 DAILY).
- **Based on your treadmill:** the pack is your best treadmill's biome (Trail Runner = Forest … Thunder Runner = Storm).
- **Never Common:** Uncommon 45%, Rare 30%, Epic 17%, Legendary 6%, Mythic 1.9%, and **0.1% a Void pack**. It rolls with the normal earned-pack luck.
- An unlocked pack you didn't take before midnight goes into your Bag on your next visit. It's never lost. If your Bag is full it waits (up to 3 packs, saved) and goes in as soon as there is room: you get one "🎁 Make room: N mystery pack(s) waiting" notice, and today's 15 minutes keep running meanwhile.

### Speed sign above every keeper
- Every keeper (and The Darkened) has a sign: **SPEED NEEDED ⚡ 2.5M**, the speed points you need to outrun it.
- It turns green with ✓ once you're fast enough (your friend boost counts). Keepers you can always outrun say **⚡ ANY**. Keepers no one can outrun yet say **TOO FAST**.

### Candy keyboard track
- Like "+1 Speed Keyboard Escape": the track is now one long keyboard of giant candy keys, 6 keys wide down the middle, from the start to The Darkened.
- Keys **press down and click** under every runner and every keeper. The letters cycle QWERTY / ASDFGH / ZXCVBN / 123456, and each biome starts with a big spacebar.
- The candy colours change row by row, starting on a different sweet in every biome.
- **It's drawn on each player's own screen** (no server work, no lag for others). Only the keys near you are real parts; the rest of the keyboard is cheap flat strips.
- Shovel holes still show and still work on top of it.
- Effects volume 0 in Settings mutes the clicks.

### Verity (quest NPC behind the market)
- A **big Verity** (24 × 34 studs, your picture) on a round stage behind the market, with a **"!"** above her. Walk up and **Talk**.
- **Her quest:** steal a Void pack from The Darkened and bring it to her. She swaps it for a **Verity pack**: same size, same coat, same weather mutation.
- **Every Void pack counts**, as many times as you like. While you hold a Void pack, her marker turns into a bouncing gold **?**.
- Her window shows how many Void packs you have, how many you've given her, and when The Darkened is back.
- The Void pack you're holding goes first, otherwise your first one. The Verity pack takes its place in your Bag and gets the new-pack rainbow ring. After a rejoin it sits with your newest packs.

### Verity pack
- **The Void pack's odds with the King seeds removed, plus the Verity seed:**

  | Seed | Chance |
  |---|---|
  | 🌟 **Verity seed** | **1%** (1/100) |
  | each of the 5 Secret seeds | 18.7% |
  | each of the 5 Cosmic seeds | 0.99% |
  | the Mech seeds, as in the Void pack: Plasma Pepper (Legendary), Holo Melon and Prism Lotus (Mythic), Holo Apple Tree (Secret), Nebula Vine (Cosmic) | 0.24%, 0.13%, 0.08%, 0.035%, 0.012% |

- No King seeds and no Crowncore Tree. Holding the pack shows these odds.

### Verity seed
- **A small glossy gold ball.** Plant it and it grows into a **giant 22-stud gold Verity fruit** sitting on a ring of 14 leaves.
- Rarity **King**. 4 h first growth, then a new fruit every 25 min. Each fruit is worth **9e10 (90B)**, about as much per hour as the best King plants.
- Its own Index tab, **VERITY**: 10 💎 halfway, 100 💎 when complete.
- Never Fruit of the Hour, and it isn't sped up or slowed by the growth pacing.

### Fix: login seed packs used old odds
- GPT's daily login seed packs (days 1–4) were made without an odds version, so they opened with the old, more generous R81 odds. New ones use today's odds. Packs already in Bags keep the old odds.

## Test commands (new)
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `mystery @name` | Show the mystery pack state. `mystery ready` unlocks it in 3 s, `mystery next` pretends it's a new day, `mystery reset` starts over |
| `verity 3 @name` | Give 1–20 Verity packs |
| `rarepacks verity @name` | A TEST Verity pack that reveals the Verity seed |
| `seeds verity @name` | Give the Verity seed |
| `odds verity` | Show the Verity pack odds |
| `eclipse 2 @name` | (old) Give Void packs, to hand to Verity |

## Test in Studio
- **Mystery pack:** go to your base: the pedestal is in the corner across from the treadmill with a black pack and a countdown. `/test mystery ready`, wait 3 s, take it: a pack of your treadmill's biome lands in the Bag. `/test mystery next` gives a new one.
- **Keeper signs:** run down the track. Each keeper's sign shows a number; `/test speed 100000000000` turns them green.
- **Keyboard:** run on the track: keys press and click under you, and under the keepers. Dig a shovel hole on it: the hole shows. Check it feels smooth on a phone (Studio device emulator).
- **Verity:** walk behind the market: Verity stands on her stage. `/test eclipse 2`, then Talk to her and press GIVE VOID PACK: the Void pack becomes a Verity pack (same slot). Open it with `/test rarepacks verity` to see the Verity seed, plant it, and watch it grow (or use the growth test commands).
- **Index:** the VERITY tab is last.
