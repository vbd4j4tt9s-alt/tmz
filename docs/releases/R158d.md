# R158d: a gift for new players, no spoilers before a pack opens, new Holo Melon and Holo Apple Tree

Needs **R158c installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158c'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158d_install.lua` (~100 KB, 17 scripts,
nothing moved or deleted). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R158d] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158d'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158d_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. Profile version stays 22 (new optional saved fields only).

## What changed
- **A gift for new players (only while the Verity event runs):** every brand-new player gets a **Verity Pack that always opens Mythic or
  better** and **2 bonus rolls**, once. It arrives when they finish (or skip) the tutorial, so the tutorial's "open the pack" step is not
  confused, with the notice **"Thanks for playing! Here's a gift"**.
  - Inside the gift pack the Verity Pack's own odds are kept for Mythic and up (almost always a Secret, as you chose).
  - Its pull is **silent**: no chat announcement, it doesn't count for BEST PULL.
  - The gift pack (and its seed, plant and fruit) can't be gifted to anyone. Old players get nothing. After the event ends, new players get
    nothing.
  - `/test starterverity @name` shows a player's gift state; `/test starterverity @name reset` (Studio only) gives it again.
- **No spoilers before a pack opens:** the seed's own effects (the Mech seed's cyan / yellow scanner in your screenshots, and the seed's
  sparkles in the Secret / Cosmic / King story scenes) no longer float round the closed pack. They appear the moment the seed shows.
  Fixed for every pack kind (Mech, Void, Verity, the biome packs, mystery, event, bonus-roll and gift packs).
- **Holo Melon and Holo Apple Tree:** the Holo Melon's fruit is now your real melon and pumpkin shapes (the cantaloupe is the melon made
  rounder and teal), and the Holo Apple Tree's fruit is the real apple. Still see-through glowing holograms that spin and switch form every
  harvest. Far fewer parts. Picture: `docs/proposals/R158b/holo_rework158d.png`.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R158d'`.

## Test in Studio
- Gift: `/test starterverity @you reset`, rejoin, finish or skip the tutorial: the notice, the Verity Pack in your Bag, 2 bonus rolls ready.
  Open it: Mythic or better, no chat line. Try to gift it: refused.
- Open a Mech pack (and a Secret / Cosmic / King story scene): nothing floats round the pack before it opens.
- Plant a Holo Melon / Holo Apple Tree: the new fruit shapes. **Check the melon's stripes** (they rely on the mesh colours showing on a
  glowing part; if Roblox ignores them, the melon is a plain see-through melon with rings).
