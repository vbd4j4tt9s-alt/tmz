# R158b: dropped packs are easy to see, no bat swing sound, holes sound like dirt

Needs **R158 installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158b_install.lua` (~62 KB, 7 scripts,
nothing moved or deleted). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R158b] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158b'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158b_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. No saved-data change.

## What changed
- **Dropped packs stand out.** When a pack drops on the track, it gets an outline you can see through walls (slowly changing white to
  gold), a gold diamond above it that says **DROPPED PACK** and how far away it is, and a slim gold beam of light. Roblox can only draw
  a few outlines at a time, so the nearest 4 drops get the outline (3 in Fast Mode); every drop gets the diamond. Fast Mode: no beam,
  no pulse. Everything goes away the moment the pack is picked up or goes back. Picture: `docs/proposals/R158/dropped_pack158.png`.
- **Bats:** no sound when you swing. Only hitting someone makes a sound (the slap).
- **Holes:** digging a hole plays your planting dirt sound (the one the garden shovel uses). Covering a hole sounds the same as before.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R158b'`.

## Test in Studio
- Get caught while carrying a pack (or bat someone who carries one): the dropped pack has the outline, the diamond and the beam; walk
  away and the diamond counts the distance; pick it up and it all goes away.
- Swing the bat at nothing: no sound. Hit someone: the slap.
- Dig a hole on the track: the dirt sound.
