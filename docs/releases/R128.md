# R128: shorter lights-out, hotbar fits beside the HUD, steady biome particles, held effects keep up, keepers land their swings

Needs R127 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R128_install.lua` (~40 KB, 13 scripts, 1 new).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R128] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R128_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

Install order if none are in yet: R124, R125, R126, R127, then R128.

## What changed

**The Darkened's lights-out is 3 s shorter**
- The lights now stay fully dark for 4 s (was 7 s). The whole effect is about 7 s: the flicker, the dark, then the lights coming back.

**Hotbar fits beside the timers**
- On computer screens the hotbar now shrinks a little when that lets the balances (left) and the boosts and timers (right) sit at the bottom beside it, instead of being pushed up above it.

  | Screen | Slot size |
  |---|---|
  | 1920×1080 | 82 px, unchanged |
  | 1600×900 | 75 px |
  | 1536 wide (a 1920 screen at 125% scaling, like your screenshot) | 69 px |

- It never goes below the pre-R127 size (66 px).
- On 1440-wide and narrower windows the side HUD only fits beside the bar below 66 px, so those keep the R127 layout (big slots, side HUD above the bar), as they had before R127 too.
- Phones are unchanged.

**Biome particles are really there now**
- Forest pollen, Jungle fireflies and mist, Desert sand, Snow, Lava embers, Crystal shimmer and Storm rain used to be stuck to a box that followed your camera. They moved with your view and jittered when you walked.
- Now each particle stays where it appears, so you walk through them.
- New particles appear around you and a bit ahead when you run. Snow and rain appear far enough ahead that you can't outrun them.
- The global rain and blizzard weather got the same fix.
- **Performance:** the same emitters and the same particle rates as before, so there are no extra particles. The only extra work is moving one invisible box each frame.
- Running very fast no longer wipes the particles. A teleport (BASE / TRACK) still clears them.

**Effects on held packs and seeds keep up when you run**
- The orbits, rings and floating bits around a carried item used to be moved only 20–30 times a second, so they trailed behind a runner. Now they move every frame while the item is carried. Items lying on the ground keep the cheaper rate. Covered:
  - weather and Mech effects;
  - pack rarity auras and orbits, plus the Mech pack rig;
  - Void Pack rings and debris;
  - held fruit effects.

**Keepers land the swings they start**
- **The problem:** a keeper started its swing up to 9–16 studs away (its full reach). The hit only counted if its body actually touched you, so a keeper right next to you could swing again and again without hitting.
- **Now:** a keeper only starts its swing when it is close, at 60% of its old reach:

  | Forest | Jungle | Desert | Snow | Lava | Crystal | Storm |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 5.4 | 5.4 | 7.2 | 6 | 9.6 | 8.4 | 9.6 |

  These are studs from the keeper's centre. A swing it starts lands if you are still within that range (+2 studs) at the hit frame, or if the bodies touch.
- Running out of range during the wind-up still dodges the hit.
- When keepers notice you and how they chase are unchanged. The reach used for the alarm, pursuit and dash is unchanged.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R128'`.

## Test in Studio
- **Lights-out:** `event spawn`. Dark for about 4 s, back to normal after about 7 s.
- **Hotbar:** check the timers and balances are at the bottom beside the hotbar.
- **Particles:** walk and run through Forest and Jungle. The pollen and fireflies should stay put, not slide with the camera.
- **Held effects:** hold a pack or seed with a ring or orbit (`weather rain all` gives seeds and packs weather effects) and run. The effect should stay on the item.
- **Keepers:** steal a pack and let a keeper catch up. It should land its swing instead of repeating it. Running away during the wind-up still escapes.
