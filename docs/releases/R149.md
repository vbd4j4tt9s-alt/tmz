## What changed
### Keyboard track (your screenshots + "keyboard asmr" reference)
- **Keys about 2.7× bigger and tall**: 22 keys across the track (8.2 studs each), 4.65 studs tall with about 2 studs of side showing, big letters.
- **One look near and far**: every key at every distance is the same keycap in the same colours, out to about 1000 studs ahead on computers (620 on phones, 380 on slow devices). Only the letters fade far away. The view follows your camera, so the keys reach far ahead when you run back to base too.
- **Rendering fixed**: the real floor under the keyboard is hidden on your screen only (still solid to walk on), nothing is flush with it any more; the gaps between keys are each biome's darker shade, not black; the keyboard has finished edges.
- **Colours match each biome**: Forest grass greens, Jungle deep green / lime, Desert sand, Snow white / ice blue, Lava dark red / orange, Crystal purple / pink, Storm slate / blue-violet. Letters always readable. Each biome still starts with a cream spacebar with its name.
- **Clicks consistent**: one click sound, nearly the same pitch every time, a steady rhythm per runner (a crowd no longer silences some keys), one click per step even on a corner.
- Shovel holes, the lightning warning ring and the shovel dirt puffs show on top of the keys. Pack platforms hold their keys down.
- The camera never dips under the keys; turning the camera rebuilds the keys smoothly over a few frames (no hitch on phones).
- Phone safety switch: `ReplicatedStorage.KeyboardTrack.UseKeycapMesh = false` makes every key a plain block (same size and colours) if the keycap is too heavy on phones.

### Weather is real now (no more stopping when you turn the camera)
- Rain, thunder and blizzard fall from the sky in the world around you (not stuck to the camera): turning or moving the camera never stops them.
- Rain and thunder splash on the ground. Lightning bolts land in your base.
- Blizzard lays snow patches on the ground around you in the base: they fade in, stay while it snows, then fade out.
- The Snow biome has permanent snow patches (edges, borders, dust) that never cover shovel holes, packs or the keeper camp.
- Phones get fewer particles (rain capped at 900 live drops).

### Snow keeper (the tiger): silver-and-sapphire armour
- Helm with sapphire, cheek guards, collar, shoulder and back plates and more (38 pieces). It moves with every pose. Hit areas unchanged.

### Growth style (Grow a Garden reference, your "phase 1" OK)
- **Sprout pop**: the seedling springs up and opens its leaves as the dirt pile sinks.
- **Faster start**: 35 % of full size a quarter of the way through (was 20 %). Timings unchanged.
- **Solid leaves** that grow out from the stem (no more see-through fading).
- **Fruit ripens nicely**: starts pale green, swells a little, then turns its bright ripe colour (no muddy brown middle). Glowing fruit lights up when ripe.
- **Ripe moment**: one bounce and a few glints.
- **Picking**: the fruit floats off the plant into you, shrinking a little, and your hotbar slot flashes when it arrives. Others see it fly to you.
- Growing plants sway like ripe ones.
- **Much less work for phones**: growing plants near you were redrawn ~18,500 times a second each; now about 200.
- Lantern Fern and Amethyst Grape look exactly as before at every step. Every plant's final ripe look is unchanged.
