# R140: daily login week (day 7 = Mech pack), daily quests, friend speed boost + invite, "your plant is ready"

Needs R139 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R140_install.lua` (~95 KB, 12 scripts, 4 of them new).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R140] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R140_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed
### New buttons: 🎁 DAILY and 👥 INVITE
- Two square buttons in the top bar, right of BASE / TRACK, on computers and phones.
  - On very narrow phones (under ~370 px) they sit just under the bar on the left.
- 🎁 DAILY has a red number: login reward waiting (1) + finished quests to claim.

### Daily login week
- One claim per day. Days start at midnight UTC, the same moment for everyone.

  | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 | Day 4 | Day 5 | Day 6 | Day 7 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 💎 5 | 💎 5 | 💎 10 | 💎 10 | 💎 15 | 💎 15 | 🤖 **1 Mech pack** |

- After day 7 the week starts again at day 1.
- **Missing a day doesn't reset the week.** You continue where you left off.
  - If you want a missed day to restart the week, set `ResetIfMissed = true` in `ReplicatedStorage.DailyRewards`.
  - All the numbers are in that module.
- **The window:**
  - It opens by itself once per session when a reward is waiting (only after the tutorial).
  - Claimed days get a ✓, today's card says TODAY, and the big button says CLAIM DAY 3! (or CLAIM YOUR MECH PACK!).
  - With nothing to claim, the button counts down to the next day.
- **Gems** arrive as the usual collectable gems, like Index rewards. **The Mech pack** goes straight into the Bag and gets the R139 rainbow ring.
  - It's a free pack, not a bought one, so it opens for everyone.
  - With a full Bag, the claim waits until you make room. Nothing is lost.

### Daily quests (5 💎 each)
- **3 quests a day.** The first is always **🎒 Steal 3 packs**. The other two are picked each day from:
  - 🎁 Open 3 packs
  - 🌱 Plant 3 seeds
  - 🍎 Pick 5 fruit
  - 💰 Sell 5 fruit
- **What counts:**
  - A steal counts when the stolen pack is banked at your base. Gifts, bought packs and bonus packs don't count.
  - Every fruit counts when you sell all at once.
- A finished quest shows a notice: "✅ QUEST DONE: Steal 3 packs! Claim 💎5 in 🎁 DAILY".
- In the QUESTS tab, each quest has a progress bar and a CLAIM button once it's done. It also shows "NEW QUESTS IN 5h 11m".
- New quests come at midnight UTC, including for players who are online then.

### Friend speed boost + invite
- **Each Roblox friend in your server: +10% walk speed**, counting up to 3 friends (+30%).
  - It works everywhere you run, including runs with a pack.
  - The keepers and the anti-cheat use the same boosted speed, so it won't cause false alarms.
- When a friend joins, both players get a notice, e.g. "👥 Ben is here! Friend boost: +20% speed".
- 👥 INVITE shows a green **+20%** chip while friends are here. Tapping it opens Roblox's invite friends screen with "Friends here make you faster! 👥".

### "Your plant is ready" (needs a one-time setup, below)
- **When it sends:** a player leaves with a plant still growing. When its first fruit is ready, Roblox sends a notification: "Your Watermelon is ready…" (your text).
- **How it's sent:**
  - Any running server sends it. The ready times are shared between servers, so it works after the player's server has closed.
  - Only one server sends each one.
- **Limits:**
  - At most 1 per player every 12 hours.
  - None if the plant is ready within 5 minutes of leaving, or if the player is back in the game.
- **Who gets it** (Roblox's rules):
  - players 13+ who said yes to notifications;
  - the game asks once per session, a moment after they plant a seed after the tutorial;
  - Roblox shows that prompt at most once every 30 days.
- **Until you do the setup, nothing is sent and nobody is asked.**

#### Setup for "your plant is ready" (once)
1. **Creator Dashboard → your experience → Engagement → Notifications → create a notification string.**
   - For example: `Your {plantName} is ready to pick! 🌱`. `{plantName}` is filled in with the plant.
   - Copy its **asset ID** (a long id like `5dd7024b-68e3-…`).
2. **In Studio:** select `ServerScriptService.ChestChaseServer.SocialService`. Add a **string attribute** `MessageId` = that asset ID.
3. **Creator Dashboard → Open Cloud → API Keys → Create API key.**
   - Add the **user notifications** permission for this experience, with **write** access.
   - So game servers can use it, leave IP restrictions open (`0.0.0.0/0`).
4. **Add the key to your experience's Secrets** with the name **`PlantReadyKey`**, for the domain `apis.roblox.com`.
   - To use another name, set a string attribute `SecretName` on SocialService.
5. **Game Settings → Security → Allow HTTP Requests: ON**, then publish.
   - Roblox also requires the game to have at least 100 visits.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R140'`.
