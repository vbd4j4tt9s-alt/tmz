## What changed

**Phone layout (landscape phones and tablets), following your reference**

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Bottom-left corner | Speed, Cash and Gems as text rows. A thumb drag can start on the rows; only the small + buttons take a tap there. |
| Bottom-right corner, above the jump button | Boosts, The Darkened card, then the two timers, as one stack. The timers are plain text, with no dark cards. |
| Left middle | The MENU button (Shop / Index / Settings wheel), as before. |
| Top centre | BASE and TRACK in the top bar row, centred, as before. |
| Bottom centre | The hotbar, centred on the screen, with 5 slots on every landscape phone: 64 px on most, 56 px on 667×375, 40 px on 568×320. |

- While the Bag is open, everything except the hotbar hides, so the Bag sheet stays big (639×238 on an 844×390 phone).
- Portrait phones and computers are unchanged.
- Previews (approximate renders of the real scripts, with stand-in thumbstick and jump circles):
  - `docs/proposals/R129/phone_844x390_compare.png`, before and after;
  - `docs/proposals/R129/phone_667x375_compare.png`, before and after;
  - `docs/proposals/R129/phone_*_after.png`.

**Weather is a base thing now**
- **Real rain and snow in the base:**
  - It no longer stops when you move the camera. An old check switched it off whenever the camera passed under anything (trees, plants, props); that check is gone.
  - The drops stay in the world (R128) and you walk through them.
- **Dark sky:** during rain, thunderstorms and blizzards the base goes properly overcast.
  - The light drops, the sky and air turn dark grey (pale grey for snow) and colours dull.
  - The clouds thicken and turn dark. The place's own clouds come back exactly afterwards; if the place has no cloud layer, one is added only for the weather.
- **On the track there is no weather effect at all:** no rain, snow, lightning or dark sky. Each biome's own effects (pollen, fireflies, sand, embers…) keep showing, also during weather.
- **Gameplay is unchanged:** packs on the track can still pick up weather mutations during a weather event. Say if you want that limited to the base too.

**Mutation highlights**
- The pack glow and your plant's rainbow outline already showed through walls. The floating label over your plant now also shows from any distance (it stopped at 260 studs).
