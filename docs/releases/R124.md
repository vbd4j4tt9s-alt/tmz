# R124: shovel, hide-in bushes, bonus roll odds and look, keeper sounds, ruby gems, refresh wall, performance

Needs R123 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R124_install.lua` (~78 KB, 20 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R124] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R124_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed

**Shovel**
- The cooldown is 3 s (was 8 s).
- Clicking during the cooldown shows only the time left, e.g. "2s".
- The permanent "Click the ground to dig a hole (1/4)…" line is gone.
- When you take the shovel out on the track, "Dig holes on the ground to trap pack thieves!" fades in, then fades away after 4 s.
  - It shows again at most once every 30 s.

**Bushes you can hide in (Forest and Jungle)**
- All 8 track bushes (3 Forest, 5 Jungle) are now 7.5 studs tall and at least 9 studs wide, so a player fits inside.
- They stay on the ground, and you walk straight into them.
- The bush you are in turns see-through for you only. Everyone else sees a solid bush.
- Other players' names are hidden while they are inside a bush.
- A player carrying a pack still shows the carry nameplate, so a stolen pack can't be hidden.

**Biome title**
- The biome name fades in, holds for about 2 s, then fades away. It no longer pops off.

**Treadmill bonus rolls**
- A roll is ready every **6 minutes** on the treadmill (was 10).
- New odds:

  | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic | Secret |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 43.5% | 28.6% | 17.2% | 8.1% | 2% | 0.5% | 0.1% |

  - Secret is the Void Pack ("the cosmic pack"), always a Storm Void Pack, from any treadmill.
  - The other four keep their old 38:25:15:7 shape (owner set only Legendary, Mythic and Secret).
- The roll window:
  - Purple framed panel with a gold ribbon title, corner studs and twinkling dots.
  - Legendary cards have a gold sunburst and a shine on the border.
  - Mythic cards have flames and a pulsing border.
  - Secret cards have a starfield and a silver shimmer.
  - On a Legendary, Mythic or Secret result, the card's border and the panel's border light up and pulse. There is no full-window flash.
  - The text and the CLOSE / ROLL AGAIN buttons no longer overlap, and the buttons are centred.
  - The "Packs from: Forest, Jungle…" line is removed.
- The owner `bonus ready` reply is shortened, so it no longer runs over the window.

**Keepers**
- The Forest keeper's overhead hammer and the Jungle keeper's chest-beat slam play sound 73468358342062 at the frame the hit lands.
  - On a catch the old snap no longer plays on top of it.
- Punches, swipes and pushes (Desert, Snow, Lava, Crystal, Storm) are unchanged.
- The Darkened's catch plays sound 119010321306307 with a purple sci-fi impact:
  - a glowing shell and beam;
  - purple rings and sparks;
  - a purple screen edge if you are the one caught.
- When a keeper hits, and how far it reaches, are unchanged.

**King rarity border**
- The corner gems are polished rubies in gold settings, with a highlight that twinkles.

**Refresh wall**
- The moon and countdown are centred for every number.
- "REFRESHING" sits underneath with three dots that loop: . .. … (blank).
- A soft inner border and a few faint stars dress the white wall.

**Performance**
- Server biome particles (snow, embers, smoke, crystal motes) follow the graphics level:
  - half rate on medium graphics or Reduced Motion;
  - off in FastMode or low graphics;
  - off when more than 320 studs from the camera.
- Plant top prompts: other players' gardens are re-checked every 2 s instead of every 0.15 s.
- The track-hole hint no longer counts your holes 4 times a second.
- The biome title no longer uses a CanvasGroup, which needs GPU memory.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R124'`.

## Test in Studio (Test > Clients and Servers, 2 players)
- **Shovel:** take it out on the track (the tip fades), dig, then click again at once. You should see "2s" and be able to dig again after 3 s.
- **Bushes:** walk into a Forest or Jungle bush. It should go see-through for you. Your alt should see a solid bush and no name.
- **Bonus:** `bonus progress 5:50 @me`, then get on the treadmill; a roll is ready after 10 s. Roll and check the window.
  - `bonus ready 2 @me` to see ROLL AGAIN.
- **Keepers:** get caught by the Forest and Jungle keepers to hear the slam. `event spawn`, then get caught by The Darkened for the purple impact.
- **King border:** `seeds all @me`, open the inventory and look at a King seed.
- **Refresh wall:** `refreshpacks`, then look at the wall from the safe zone.
- **Biome title:** run from Forest into Jungle.
