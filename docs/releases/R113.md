# R113: BASE/TRACK buttons, keeper polish, inventory fixes, performance

Needs R112 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R113_install.lua` (~120 KB).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL into the Command Bar, Enter, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.
Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R113_Backup.Installer)("undo")` (undo R113 before R112).
The economy rebalance is NOT in this release (waiting for owner decisions: `docs/proposals/R113_economy_proposal.md`).

## What changed (19 scripts)
- **BASE / TRACK buttons** beside MENU: teleport home, or to the front of the track (Forest start). 4 s cooldown; refused while
  carrying a stolen pack, ragdolled, on a treadmill, opening a pack, seated; TRACK refused during a biome refresh. Anti-cheat aware.
- **Keepers:** head/jaw look at the target, wake roar, speed lean, tail lag, sleep breathing + stir, catch taunts, footfall dust and
  shake, breath and biome particles, glowing eyes, Zzz, material pass and small accents. Combat timing/reach/damage unchanged.
- **Inventory:** no flat icons when scrolling fast (cards recycled, pictures pre-built); category tabs moved outside the panel
  with real pictures; nicer fallback icons for low graphics.
- **Performance:** removed three hot spots (EconomyClient reacting to every new workspace object, GiantVisualSafety redundant writes,
  treadmill gain attribute set every frame).
- `Config.Version = 'V149 R113'`.
