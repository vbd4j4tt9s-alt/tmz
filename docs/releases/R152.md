# R152: new keepers, polished pack openings, castle hub, free Void Pack giveaway, keyboard fixes, 5-second tutorial, game text in your voice

Needs **R151 installed** (your parts 1, 2b, 3, 4). **Three installers, pasted one after the other, all three before you press Play:**
1. `installers/R152_install_part1.lua` (~339 KB, 35 scripts: keepers, keyboard): wait for **`[R152 part 1] Installed.`**
2. `installers/R152_install_part2.lua` (~300 KB, 33 scripts: pack opening, Verity, hotbar, money): wait for **`[R152 part 2] Installed.`**
3. `installers/R152_install_part3.lua` (~338 KB, 113 scripts: hub, giveaway, tutorial, game text, the loading fix): wait for **`[R152 part 3] Installed.`**

Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first** (an open script with unsaved edits makes the installer refuse), paste ALL of each into the Command Bar, Enter. Then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.
- The parts only work as a set: don't Play or publish until all three are in. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R152'` after part 3 (`'V150 R152p1'`, `'p2'` in between).
- A part refuses if the one before it isn't in ("Config is not the version this update was built for. Nothing changed."): paste them in order.
- `BackgroundMusic` is not touched (you edited it by hand); the Darkened chase change is a new small script.

Undo (newest first): `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R152p3_Backup.Installer)("undo")`, then `p2`, `p1` the same way.

## Before you publish (checklist)
1. **Game Settings → Security → "Allow Mesh / Image APIs" ON.** The new keepers, the flat Verity pack and the pack-opening textures (planets, nebulae, carpet) are built by the game itself. If it's off, the keepers stay as R151, the Verity pack uses the plain flat sachet and the reveals use plain parts. Nothing breaks.
2. **Restart every server after publishing** (Shut Down All Servers or Migrate To Latest Update). An old R151 server would save over the new "can't be gifted" mark on giveaway packs.
3. **Free Void Pack count:** live servers count down from 500 together (saved data `VoidGiveaway152`). Studio uses its own separate count, so testing never uses up real packs. To test with real saved data in Studio turn on **Game Settings → Security → "Enable Studio Access to API Services"**. A *published* test place in the same game would share the live count, so test in Studio only.
4. Optional, outside the game: the "your plant is ready" phone notification text is in your Creator Dashboard. A suggestion in your voice: `Ur {plantName} is ready to pick! 🌱`.
5. Still from R151: the two tree models didn't load in your log ("User is not authorized to access Asset"). To use them, "Get Model" on both store pages with the game owner's account, or drop them into `ReplicatedStorage.HubTreeTemplates151` by hand. Until then the hub uses the built studded trees.

Profile version stays 22 (two new optional saved fields: `GiftLocked` on giveaway packs, `TestBoosts` for your test boots; an R151 server ignores them).

## What changed
### Pack opening (top priority)
- **Plays every time:** 14 reasons it sometimes didn't play are fixed (a module loading too late, a failed start, one error stopping the loop for the session, a pack type with no reveal, a fast second pack, death mid-open...). A stress test opens 240 random packs with errors thrown in; every one plays to the end.
- **Sound on the beat:** one clock for sound and picture; every hit lands within 0.01 s of its frame at any frame rate. The King's crown and fanfare are in sync.
- **Quieter:** every reveal is softer than R151 (Secret and King peaks about 5-7 dB down), nothing clips, at most 10 sounds at once, music dips during a reveal.
- **Smooth:** eased moves with no snaps or jumps, a soft whoosh on every flight.
- **Better looking:** layered sky beams that slam down from the sky (core, glow, swirling streaks, shockwave rings, dust, cracks; gold for King), textured planets and nebulae, carpet, banners and stained glass drawn by the game.

### Hub
- No fountain, garden entrances, paintings, corner towers, BASE banners, signposts or red track disc; 24 trees instead of 62.
- **Castle look:** chess-rook battlements along every wall and a rook-tower castle gate to the track (speed keys kept).
- **Corner stands:** the fruit-of-the-hour pedestal 3x bigger with the winning seed / fruit spinning on top (its glow on the seed), and the winner's avatar 25 studs tall dancing next to it. No billboard.
- **Free Void Pack pedestal** in the middle of the plaza: one per player, 500 for the whole game, the number above it counts down live in every server, then ALL CLAIMED. These packs can't be gifted.
- Fruit of the hour: no glass tube. Z-fighting fixed everywhere (hub, keyboard, keepers, reveals).

### Keepers
- **The new rev 6 models** for all 8 keepers, built by the game at server start (no uploads), with two faces (asleep / chasing) and their effects: dragon flames, colossus lightning and rain, Darkened wisps, leaves, frost, the knight's slash trail. Gameplay, speed and catches are unchanged.

### Keyboard
- Keys and letters now show **behind you and far ahead** (about 530 studs ahead, 230 behind).
- **Every normal key** plays your sound 73942179280083; the spacebars keep the click. Louder and heard further.
- No keys inside the oasis, lava pools or under big props.

### Inventory and money
- **Fixed: sometimes the default Roblox inventory showed** (a module loaded too late and crashed the hotbar and ~15 other scripts). Every client script now waits for the game to finish loading.
- **One click = one action** in the hotbar (no more double clicks), and packs no longer drop from your hand on a quick re-equip.
- **Money flies to your balance by itself:** selling, gem converter, cash bundles, Index and daily gems. No hovering.

### Sounds, text, tutorial, Verity
- No hotbar sounds, no treadmill sound; The Darkened's chase music starts at 0:22.
- **Tutorial in 5 seconds:** one idea per step, a beam / ring / pressing hand on the exact thing, a few words: GO TO THE TRACK! → GRAB A PACK! → RUN HOME!! → OPEN IT! → PLANT IT! → GET FASTER! (moves on by itself, NICE!! pop, free pack at the end).
- **All game text in your voice** (252 lines: "NOT ENOUGH CASH!", "MAKE ROOM IN UR BAG FIRST!", "Ur Gems are on the way!"...). Verity's quest texts have more personality.
- **Verity pack:** a clean flat pouch, no leftover design, crisp face on both sides.

### Smaller fixes
- Your own test boots and test seeds count as test (no chat announcement, no hub board).
- BEST PULL keeps syncing between servers around plant updates; a backlog of plant notifications no longer delays the daily reset; a failed pack shape no longer hints the next rare pack.
- Performance patch: the same look and sound, far fewer updates per frame (King opening on phones 395 → 144, hub looking away 295 → 6).
