## What changed
### Pack opening and big pulls
- **Secret / Cosmic / King story reveals** (Sol's RNG style, the seed is the star and floats down at the end): Secret = void vault, Cosmic = space, King = palace and crown on the pack, with your sounds.
- **Common to Mythic** got reveals of the same quality (no big quality gap), and **every pack opening has more suspense**.
- **Chat announcements only** (no banners): Legendary+ in your server, Secret+ in every server, sent **after the puller's own reveal shows the seed** (nobody spoils it). Packs from owner test commands never announce.
- **Hub displays:** giant **BEST PULL TODAY** and **BIGGEST FRUIT TODAY** (fruit type rotates daily) in the back corners, with the winner's avatar, shared across servers; a new record is announced in chat.

### Hub and world
- **Seed Festival Square:** dressed walls with towers, murals and banners, a gate at the track, streets, base arches with owner names, lamps, benches, fountain, butterflies.
- **Studded trees**, just trees (no fruit), your two tree models used when they load; **no collision** on any tree.
- **Cloudy sky:** the default weather alternates Clear (~8 min) and Cloudy (~6 min); Cloudy dims the light and the lamps and lanterns glow warm.
- **Blizzard snow** across the whole hub in fewer, bigger drifts; **rain** splashes only some drops (cheaper on phones).
- **New chase music** (your GTA track) and a new theme for The Darkened (your "intense" track).

### Keyboard track
- **Letters on every key** (before, one sideways column showed), **keys press down properly**, and FOREST / DESERT run across the track, parallel to the SAFE ZONE line.
- **No missing keys around you** after running back, looking sideways/down or teleporting.

### Treadmills
- Same shape, plus a **moving belt pattern per biome**, glowing trims, studs, small corner lamps, a **"+N/step"** label and an **upgrade sign** (next level, gain, price; MAX LEVEL).
- **5 steps a second** instead of 6: the same speed per second, every "+N/step" is a round multiple of 5 (+20, +80, +400, +2K, +12K, +80K, +600K).
- **Speed popups** feel like your video: pop, fling out in a fan, stop, fade (10 a second), our colours; numbers of 1,000+ read 1K / 1M.

### Fruit, Verity, packs
- No white shine dots on any fruit; the Verity fruit has one face; **every fruit and plant floats into you** when picked (65 of 65 seeds).
- Verity: no mouth, her ball bounces with her voice; greeting cut at 2.35 s (tune with `/test verityvoice`); the Verity pack uses the real chip-bag pouch in pure yellow.
- Packs: giant packs no longer leave floating bars; **every new pack rolls one of 6 chip-bag shapes** and keeps it everywhere (shop / market pictures use the normal shape).

### Menus and notices
- The red notification badges (INDEX, DAILY, tabs, MENU) are whole circles now (they were cut off).
- Esc menu: one big rainbow line "🌱 Plants grow offline".
- Fixed: the daily login window never opened by itself for returning players; plant-ready notifications were lost on server shutdown, in busy games and for a second plant in a day.

## Known small issues (next update)
- Owner-granted boots (`/test boots`) and seeds don't count as "test", so the owner's own pulls with them can be announced / take the hub boards.
- A server whose "fruit of the day" differs from other servers (only around a future update that adds plants) can stop syncing BEST PULL for that day.
- A big backlog of plant notifications can delay that server's daily reset by a minute or two.
- A very quick unequip / re-equip of a pack while its shape loads can drop it from your hand.
- A failed pack-shape bake names that design in a status attribute (could hint the next rare pack).
