# R158e: the wordless tutorial (arrows and pointing only), a 10-second first fruit, the Verity gift at spawn

Needs **R158d installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158d'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158e_install.lua` (~119 KB, 11 scripts,
nothing moved or deleted). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R158e] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158e'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158e_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. Profile version stays 22.

## What changed
- **The tutorial has no words.** Only arrows, rings, pointing hands and icons, 10 steps, each moving on by itself:
  1. you spawn: a big red arrow points at the packs (and a red arrow at the screen edge when they are out of view);
  2. the TRACK button (arrow, ring, pressing hand);
  3. steal a pack (arrow over it + your key as an icon; then the arrow points home);
  4. open any pack (the clicking mouse / tapping hand);
  5. the BASE button;
  6. plant (arrow on your dirt + the pressing hand);
  7. your first fruit grows in **10 seconds** (a ring timer over it). Only once, only for the plant from the tutorial;
  8. harvest; 9. sell (the arrow to the market, then Sell crops / Sell all);
  10. the treadmill: 🏃 ➜ ⚡ (faster) and 🕒 6:00 ➜ 🎁 with an arrow to BONUS ROLL (a bonus roll every 6 minutes).
  Skip is the ⏭ icon (tap twice). Picture: `docs/proposals/R158e/tutorial158e.png`.
- Players halfway through the old tutorial continue at the matching step; finished players stay finished.
- **The Verity gift** (while the Verity event runs) now arrives the moment a new player first spawns, with "Thanks for playing! Here's
  a gift". It doesn't count as the tutorial's steal.
- The free Forest pack at the end, the Void pedestal unlock and "no replay" work as before.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R158e'`.
