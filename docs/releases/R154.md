## What changed
### Pack opening
- **The seed waits for u.** After the reveal the result stays on screen (no timer) with a pulsing **"click to collect!"** ("tap to collect!" on
  phone). Click / tap anywhere, Enter, gamepad B or R2 and the seed **flies into its hotbar slot** (the stack it joins, else the first free slot, else
  the Bag button), pops, the slot flashes and the pickup sound plays. A Secret / Cosmic / King scene fades back to the world first, then the seed flies.
- That click never plants, digs or swings anything.
- **Nothing is ever lost:** the waiting seed collects itself when u open another pack, die, get ragdolled or flung, a chase starts, u teleport, walk
  onto / off the track or far away, or open a shop or menu. With "Skip pack animations" on it still ends on the waiting result.
- The seed (and its weather / Mech effects) stays out of ur hand and hotbar until it lands. Other players see what they saw before.

### Luck
- **The 4 Leaf Clover's x2 is on every pack:** world and stolen packs, bonus roll, daily and login mystery packs, the mystery pedestal, the starter pack,
  and now also the Void, Verity and Mech packs (which ignore boots).
- **The luck cap doubles with the clover:** Thunder Boots + clover = **x100M** (was stuck at x50M). The per-tier limits inside a pack still apply
  (King at most 1% of a pack, Cosmic 5%, Secret 25%, Mythic 45%), so at Thunder Boots the clover moves 32 of the 42 biome packs, not all (see "Your call").
- **No pack is (almost) one seed any more:** when one seed would take more than 80% of a pack, it keeps 80%, the others get at least 19%, and 1% goes
  to the next rarer seed. Forest Mythic: Sunflower / Elderbloom both stay possible at every luck. Packs at 80% or less roll exactly as before.
- The odds shown on the Index, reveal card, chat and hub sign don't change (they show each seed's one fixed 1/N, as in R153).
- `/test rarepacks` gives a random seed of each rarity (from any biome), so `rarepacks mythic` isn't always Elderbloom.

### Hub
- **Garden bed sides:** u walk up onto every bed and pad from every side and corner (invisible gentle ramps all the way round, also over the soil strip
  at the front of each garden). No jump needed anywhere.
- **Trampolines fill the whole brick circle** and bounce anywhere on it; u walk straight onto them (low invisible ramp). **The third nook** (the back
  lane's round bed + stone bench) is a trampoline too. If ur trampoline asset loads, its look is sized to the circle as well.
- **Tidier hub:** the flower beds, the benches beside them, the pebbles and their butterflies are gone; lamp posts 26 -> 12 (the 8 that light up and the
  4 holding bunting / starting the garden walks). Bushes, trees, flags and wall lanterns stay.
- **Warmer, brighter lamps whenever it's dim:** Cloudy, Rain, Thunderstorm, Blizzard and The Darkened's lights-out: about 2x brighter, wider, and a
  warm orange (heads glow too). Back to normal when it clears. (The game has no night: the hub clock stays at 2 pm.)
- **No z-fighting left in the hub** (98 flickering spots -> 0: the lawn patches, fence sills, a lamp arm, blizzard snow drifts).

### UI
- **Notification bubbles:** the number / "!" / "9+" fills the bubble (it was tiny).
- **Speed popups:** 20% smaller, one steady size the whole time, no more popups cut short or jumping to another place during lag.

### Less lag (looks the same except these two)
- **B1:** parts the game's scripts build under 1.5 studs cast no shadow (hub plaza on PC: 3,186 -> 2,315 shadow casters near u). Bigger parts, the saved
  map and characters keep their shadows.
- **B3:** phones (graphics tier 2): the keyboard track's letters are drawn a bit softer (12 px a stud) and 56 rows ahead (keys 2,288 -> 1,892, letter
  memory 32.8 -> 22.4 MB). PC and tier 1 unchanged.
- Studio only: the "waiting for item pictures" spam is one summary line every 5 s.
