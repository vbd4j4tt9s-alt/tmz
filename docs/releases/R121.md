# R121: gift buttons, 10-minute x2 boost, performance pass

Needs R120 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R121_install.lua` (~91 KB).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R121_Backup.Installer)("undo")` (undo R121 before R120).

**After installing, paste your product ids** - full list and steps: `docs/proposals/shop_R121/PRODUCT_IDS.md`
(15 Number attributes: `SpeedBoost10ProductId` on `ReplicatedStorage.PremiumPricing`, 14 `Gift...ProductId` on
`ReplicatedStorage.GiftProducts`). Until an id is set, its button shows **SOON** and does nothing.

## What changed (26 scripts, 4 new)

- **Gift buttons** on every Robux product (5 cash, 5 speed, Mech pack x1/x5/x10, the boost). Pick a player, buy the gift
  product, the server gives the reward to them and tells both players. Paid gifts are saved before Roblox is told the
  purchase worked (never lost): if the receiver left, the buyer keeps it ("Send gift · N ready") to send later; offline
  receivers get it when they rejoin.
- **10-minute x2 boost** (the "DOUBLE Your SPEED" banner): x2 treadmill training for 10 minutes, +10 min per purchase
  (self-buys up to 60 min), keeps running offline and after rejoining, stacks with the x2 Speed pass (x4). Countdown on
  the banner and in the bottom-right timers. The permanent x2 Speed pass is unchanged.
- **Performance pass (8 fixes, same visuals and gameplay):** keeper notice cleanup no longer rescans every frame
  (server), distant growing plants update less often, closed menus stop checking themselves, keeper voice writes only on
  change, drop countdown once a second, lava / hit flash / track arrows reuse work. Details: `docs/proposals/perf_R121/REPORT.md`.
- Narrow phone cards: the Gems price moves to its own row above [Gift | Robux].
- `Config.Version = 'V149 R121'`.
