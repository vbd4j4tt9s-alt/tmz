# R138: visual tutorial + free lucky pack, clicking indicator, pull sounds, Index reward alerts

Needs R137 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R138_install.lua` (~71 KB, 13 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R138] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R138_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed
### Tutorial: clearly visual, oversimplified
Previews:
- `docs/proposals/R138/tutorial_welcome.png`
- `docs/proposals/R138/tutorial_steps.png`
- `docs/proposals/R138/tutorial_click_finish.png`

Changes:
- **Welcome:** the whole game in four pictures: 🎒 STEAL ➜ 🏠 RUN HOME ➜ 🎁 OPEN ➜ 🌱 PLANT. A short "Hey Alex! 👋", then a big pulsing **LET'S GO!**
- **Every step is one big icon, 2–3 words and one key chip.** No more sentences:
  - 🎒 **STEAL A PACK** `[E] HOLD`. The key changes for each device: `[STEAL]` on phones, `[X]` on gamepads.
  - 🏠 **RUN HOME!** ➜ FOLLOW THE ARROWS
  - 🎁 **OPEN IT** `[CLICK] YOUR PACK` (the arrow still points at it in the hotbar), then **KEEP CLICKING!** once it's in your hand
  - 🌱 **PLANT IT** ➜ FOLLOW THE ARROWS
- **A row of step icons** 🎒 ➜ 🏠 ➜ 🎁 ➜ 🌱 ➜ 💰 replaces the dots and "STEP 1/5". Done steps turn green with a ✓, and the current one is big and gold.
- **The tips are now 3 picture slides (5 s each, NEXT to skip):**
  - 🌱 ➜ 🍎 ➜ 💰 GROW · PICK · SELL;
  - ZOOM HOME `[BASE]`, with that button ringed;
  - ZOOM TO TRACK `[TRACK]`, with that button ringed.
- **Off the track:** 🏃 GO TO THE TRACK `[TRACK]`. **No packs yet:** ⏳ PACKS SOON.
- **World marker:** 😈 STEAL! / 🏠 HOME / 🌱 PLANT.
- **Finish:** 🏆 **YOU'RE READY!** with a confetti burst, the gem sound and 🎁 FREE LUCKY PACK!
- Unchanged: the red arrow trail, the hotbar pointer, the TRACK/BASE highlights, skipping (two taps), and saved progress.

### Free Lucky Forest Pack for finishing the tutorial
- **What:** one free **Forest pack with 2x rates** ("Lucky Forest Pack"). It arrives in your Bag with a notice: "🎁 FREE Lucky Forest Pack! 2x luck 🍀".
- **2x rates:** every chance above Common is doubled for that pack:
  - Uncommon 1/4 → **1/2**;
  - Rare (each) 1/16 → **1/8**;
  - Legendary 1/60 → **1/30**;
  - Mythic 1/400 → **1/200**.
  - Secret and up double too. Boots luck still stacks on top.
- **Its tooltip** says 🍀 2x LUCK and lists the doubled odds.
- **Who gets it:** once per account, for doing the steps (steal, open, plant).
  - Skipping only the last slides still counts. Skipping at the start doesn't.
  - Replaying the tutorial doesn't give a second one. The finish card then says HAVE FUN instead.
- **It keeps its 2x:** through saving, loading and gifting. A save can't fake a bigger boost.

### Louder Secret, Cosmic and King reveals
- Their build-up whoosh, the rising beats and the burst now play **2–3x louder** (they were very quiet: .04–.12).
- King also gets a second, deeper hit under its fanfare.
- Legendary / Mythic and the low tiers keep their volumes.

### Clicking indicator (opening the pack)
- While the tutorial asks you to open the pack, a **mouse (👆 on phones, 🎮 on gamepads)** sits next to the pack in your hand.
- It **keeps pressing** with a ripple, and **CLICK!** / **TAP!** pulses under it.
- **Five pips** show the clicks still needed. Each click lights one with a little pop.
- It disappears the moment the pack opens. With Reduced Motion it stays still.

### Common, Uncommon and Rare pulls feel good too
- **Sounds** (you, when the seed pops out):
  - **Common:** a bubble pop.
  - **Uncommon:** a pop plus a rising note.
  - **Rare:** a sparkle and two rising notes.
  - The old quiet start chime is gone.
- **Small animations:**
  - **At the bag (everyone sees it):** a small ring in the tier colour spreads out. Uncommon adds 8 sparkles. Rare adds 14 sparkles and a little flash of light.
  - **On your screen:** 4 / 6 / 8 tiny sparkles pop out. Rare also gets a faint ring.
  - It's all over in about half a second. There's no world sound for people nearby.
- Legendary and up are unchanged.

### Index
- **Unclaimed rewards are flagged:**
  - a red count badge on the **INDEX** button;
  - a red **!** on the **MENU** button, so you see it with the menu closed;
  - a red dot on every biome tab that has something to claim.
  - Seed cash rewards and the half-way / full biome gem rewards all count. The badges pop when a new reward arrives and disappear when everything is claimed.
- **Claim sounds:** a cash reward plays a ka-ching, and gem rewards play the gem sound.
- **The empty gray box above the seed names is gone.** It was the R137 pedestal shadow.
