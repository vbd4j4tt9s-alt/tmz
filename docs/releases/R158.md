Needs **R157b installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R157b'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158_install.lua` (~295 KB, 27 scripts,
nothing moved or deleted). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R158] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158'` after it.

## What changed
### Bats
- **Your bat swings the moment you click.** No waiting for the server.
- **What your screen shows is what counts.** While the bat sweeps, your game checks where the other players are on YOUR screen and tells
  the server "I hit this player". The server checks it is fair (were you really there, facing them, close enough, no wall, one hit per
  swing, not too many swings, and only as far back in time as your real internet lag), then the hit does what it always did (knock back,
  fall down, a stolen pack drops, the pyramid pack goes back to the pyramid).
- Our test (a pretend game with fake lag, 1,500 swings per case): of the hits you see, **100 of 100 count** at every speed and every lag we
  tried. Before: about 0 of 100 once the other player runs 141 or faster.
- **The new swing:** flat at hip height like your video. The hit lands at the same moment as before (0.3 s); the whole swing is 0.85 s.
  Only your top half swings, so your legs keep running and jumping. R15 and R6.
- **Effects:** a short **white** trail while the bat sweeps, the slap at once, the star burst plus small sparks, and a tiny freeze of your
  swing at a hit. **Your camera does not shake** when you hit (the player you hit still shakes). No "SMACK!" word. Trail and sparks are off
  in Fast Mode and on slow devices.
- **Good to know:** a very fast runner can be knocked back a moment after they think they got past you. That is the price of "what you
  see is what you get" for the one who swings.

### No "SMACK" anywhere
- Getting hit by a bat while carrying a pack shows **no notice** now (the pack still drops where you can see it). "ZAP!" (lightning) and
  "CAUGHT!" stay.
- The pyramid's line is **"THE PACK WENT BACK IN THE PYRAMID"** (no SMACK).
- `/test keepersmack` still works; its help now says "hit pose".

### Track walls (every biome its own wall, nothing stamped)
- **Forest:** a log fort (logs with sharp tips, two log rails, a mossy bank, ivy strands). **Jungle:** temple ruins (mossy stone, stepped
  tops, carved faces, a glyph band, pillars, hanging vines). **Desert:** sandstone temple (flared top, painted bands, gold-tipped obelisks,
  golden winged suns, carved doors). **Snow:** ice bricks, ice pillars, a snow cap with icicles, drifts. **Lava:** dark basalt, a jagged
  top, glowing cracks, a glowing seam at the foot. **Crystal:** purple cave stone, crystal clusters on top, glowing stripes. **Storm Peaks:**
  dark slate, broken teeth, buttresses, copper lightning rods, big bolts. Plus a tower where two biomes meet.
- **No balls, blobs or drips** on any wall (as you asked: 171 pieces taken off: ivy / leaf / vine balls, moss tufts, overgrowth and rubble
  blobs, snow pillows and mounds, the 20 lava drips, the rods' glow balls). A test fails if one ever comes back.
- Spacing, sizes and heights vary along each wall from a fixed pattern, so no two stretches look the same.
- Nothing new can be stood on or bumped into: walls keep their size, height and collision. The wall decoration above the black refresh
  cover hides while the track refreshes.

### Base walls (A)
- A dark stone cap on every merlon (five close shades) and a stone coping along the whole wall top.

### Outside the track
- **Forest:** 14 big trees, **Jungle:** 29 big trees, all made from the game's own trees (the Forest oak, the jungle tree, the hub's oaks,
  poplars and palms), uneven heights, a taller and thicker back row. No temple or cliff.
- **Desert:** your pyramid (asset 9981304), far right. **Storm Peaks:** your dark mountain, two on each side (cut from 1,532 to 280 parts
  each so it doesn't lag). **Crystal:** the game's own crystal clusters as giant spires. **Snow:** your snow hills (from your folder) plus
  the game's ice trees. **Lava:** your volcano replaces the old Lava volcano on the track, with more of it outside.
- None of it casts shadows, none of it can be touched, and the server never waits for it to load (players join right away; your volcano
  appears a moment later).

### Lava biome
- **All lava streams and pools are gone** (the river, the pond, the side pools, the channels: 762 parts). Keyboard keys fill the floor where
  the lava was. Nothing in the game used them (no damage, no slow).

### Speed popups
- **1.5x bigger on a computer** (text 35 -> 53 px on a 1080p screen). Phones get a bit less (about 1.2x to 1.47x on landscape phones, the
  same on the smallest phones) so the popups don't cover the screen. Zoom works like before.

### Hub bunting
- The flags hang **on** the string now, and the string droops between the lamps. The four strings by the market's corners end on the lamp
  tops (they used to stop in the air).
