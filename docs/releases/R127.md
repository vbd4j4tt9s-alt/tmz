# R127: mutation highlights, rarer Legendary/Mythic spawns, more giant packs, bigger hotbar and Bag, The Darkened lights-out

Needs R126 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R127_install.lua` (~59 KB, 15 scripts, 2 of them new).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R127] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R127_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

Install order if none are in yet: R124, R125, R126, then R127.

## What changed

**Mutation highlights (weather: Drippy / Frosted / Charged)**
- **Packs:** when a seed pack on the track picks up a weather mutation, that pack glows in the weather's colour for **every player**, even through walls.
  - The glow pulses for 20 s.
  - It goes away if someone takes the pack or it respawns.
  - A held pack that changes glows too.
- **Plants and fruit:** when one of your plants or fruits picks up a mutation, only **you** see it outlined in **rainbow neon**.
  - A rainbow label floats over it saying what changed, e.g. "❄️ Frosted Watermelon!".
  - If only fruit changed, the fruit itself is outlined.
  - It lasts 12 s, then fades.
- **The message now names the plant**, e.g.:
  - "🌈 Your Watermelon plant and 2 fruits became Frosted!"
  - "A fruit on your Moonberry became Charged!"
  - "Your Apple, Moonberry and 2 more plants became Drippy!"
- **Limits:** at most 16 pack glows and 12 plant outlines at once, which stays under Roblox's 31-highlight limit.

**World pack spawn odds**
- Legendary is now 3% and Mythic 1%. They were 10% and 5%.
- The other tiers keep their old shape:

  | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic | Legendary | Mythic |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 42.92% | 28.24% | 16.94% | 7.9% | 3% | 1% |

- The guarantees stay: a Legendary every 5th refresh, a Mythic every 10th.
- Treadmill bonus rolls keep their own odds from R124.

**Giant packs are more common**

| Size | 0.5x | 1x | 1.5x | 2.5x | 3.5x | 5x | 7.5x | 10x | 15x | 20x | 25x |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now | 3% | 76.5% | 14% | 4.5% | 1.4% | 0.45% | 0.1% | 0.035% | 0.01% | 0.004% | 0.001% |
| Was | 3% | 80.8% | 13% | 2.8% | 0.35% | 0.04% | 0.008% | 0.002% | 0.0007% | 0.0002% | 0.0001% |

- 5x or bigger: 0.6% (about 12 times more often).
- 10x or bigger: 0.05% (about 17 times more often).
- The same table is used for world packs, Mech packs and bonus rolls.

**Bigger hotbar and Bag**
- Hotbar slots are 82 px on computers (was 66), 72 px on narrow windows (was 60), and up to 64 px on phones (was 56). Small phones still shrink them to keep 5 slots.
- The Bag sheet is up to 1080×740 (was 900×620), its item cards are 124 px (was 104), and its category tabs are up to 84 px (was 72).
- On phones the screen is the limit, so the Bag is about the same size there.
- The left round menu button is unchanged.

**The Darkened's arrival: lights out**
- The lights flicker off. Then the air goes black a short way from the camera: you only see things close to you, and everything further away is black.
- A soft lantern glow lights the ground right around your own character. Each player only sees their own lantern.
- After about 7 s fully dark, the lights come back over 2.5 s. The whole effect is about 10 s.
- Reduced Motion: no flicker and a slightly lighter dark.
- The old black screen with a hole in it is gone.
- The darkness goes through the game's own biome lighting blend, so the normal lighting always comes back exactly. That holds even if you respawn, a second Darkened arrives, or the script reloads.

**Owner test command**
- `weather rain all` (or `thunder all` / `blizzard all`): every pack, plant and fruit that is out in the weather changes on that event. Use it to see the highlights.
- Plain `weather rain` keeps the real 2% odds.
