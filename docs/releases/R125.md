# R125: hold E to steal, biome titles redone, slower plants, tutorial travel buttons, offline-growth card, Featured logo

Needs R124 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R125_install.lua` (~60 KB, 13 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R125] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R125_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed

**Hold E to steal**
- World seed packs and the Void Packs need a 1 s hold of E (or X / the STEAL button).
- Grabbing a dropped pack needs a 0.5 s hold, because a drop only lasts 5 s.
- Both times are `Config.StealHoldSeconds` and `Config.DroppedStealHoldSeconds`.
- The tutorial now says "Hold E to steal", "Hold X to steal" or "Hold STEAL to grab".

**Biome titles (the logos had vanished)**
- Redone. The biome logo sits in a round medallion with a coloured disc, a rim, an inner ring and a shine.
- Next to it: "ENTERING" above the biome name, on a soft pill, with an accent underline with diamond ends and two twinkles.
- It drops in, holds about 2.4 s, then rises and fades away.
- The logo is drawn the way it was before R124, which is why it had disappeared.

**Plants grow slower (your answers)**
- Forest plants, Common and Uncommon plants keep their times. So do the paid Mech plants: I didn't change Robux items without asking.
- All others now grow in their rarity's range. Within a rarity the fastest takes the low end and the slowest the high end:

  | Rare | Legendary | Mythic | Secret | Cosmic | King |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 15–30 min | 45–60 min | 1–1.5 h | 2–2.5 h | 2.5–3 h | 3–4 h |

- Fruit regrowing on the same plant slows by the same amount.
- Fruit is worth more, so each plant keeps about half of its old cash per hour (your "half-way" choice). It is never worth less than before.
  - One exception: Pulsar Starfruit stops at the safe maximum value, 9×10¹⁰. A harvest's value has to stay under the save limit of 9×10¹² even after the ×100 size/mutation/weather cap. It keeps 17% instead of half.
- Plants already in the ground finish their current growth on the old timer. New plantings and later regrowth use the new times, and every harvest from now on uses the new values.
- The Double Plant Growth pass still halves all of these.

**Tutorial**
- Off the track, step 1 now says "Click TRACK at the top to zoom to the track!".
  - A yellow ring pulses around the real TRACK button, with a bouncing arrow under it.
  - On the track it switches back to "Follow the red arrows…".
- Two new tips teach BASE ("zoom home") and TRACK ("zoom back to the track"), each highlighting its button.
- The first tip now says plants grow even when you're offline.
- A "NICE! ✔" stamp pops on the card each time a step is done.
- Saved tutorial progress works exactly as before.

**Plants grow offline (Esc menu)**
- While the Roblox menu is open, a green card at the bottom of the screen says "YOUR PLANTS GROW WHILE YOU'RE OFFLINE".
  - Under it: "Your 6 plants keep growing - come back to harvest!", or a tip to plant before leaving if you have no plants.

**Shop**
- The Featured button has its own logo, a gold star with a sparkle, in the same style as the crown. It used to reuse the Mech gem.

**Shovel tip**
- It now reads "Dig holes on the ground to trap players!".
