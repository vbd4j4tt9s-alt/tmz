## What changed
### Pack opening
- **The full animation plays every time.** It used to get shorter when you opened packs within 3 s of each other ("stops after 2 / 3"). Secret,
  Cosmic and King scenes play in full every single time.
- **Skip:** click / tap the world (or Enter, gamepad B / R2): the first press jumps to the hit (the hit sound and the result still play), the next
  press closes the card. A skip click never plants ur seed, digs or uses the shovel.
- **New setting "Skip pack animations"** (off by default): short version for Common–Mythic, just the result card for Secret / Cosmic / King.
- **One fixed rarity per seed everywhere** (Index, reveal card, chat, hub sign): a Polar Starbloom from a Void pack shows its original 1/1M, not 1/101.

### Bonus roll, clover, daily
- **Bonus roll button:** ur green "?" pack instead of the gift, green and gold, the yellow fill reaches both ends, and the countdown is on the
  button: NEXT 5:41 → ALMOST THERE! (last 30 s) → BONUS READY! / OPEN IT! → READY!. No more NEXT ROLL sign over ur head, no "Ur pack is already
  in ur bag!" line, and the **Secret (Void) pack rolls past on every reel**.
- **You can roll and open packs on a treadmill** (opening a pack used to end ur training).
- **4 Leaf Clover pass:** x2 luck on every pack u open, 999 gems or 999 Robux, ur clover icon.
- **Daily quests:** each pays a **mystery pack** (dark pack on a white tile), rolled like the bonus roll for ur best treadmill (never Void, Mech or
  Verity); all 3 done → **+2 gems** (a 4th row to claim). Login week: gem days unchanged, pack days are mystery packs, **day 7 is a Void pack**.
- **Index gem rewards halved** (halfway 5, completion 10, Mech / Verity sets 50).
- **Gems column** in the player list (Speed, Cash, Gems).

### Hub and track
- **Hub giants dance on every player's screen** (no shaking, floating or freezing), one big label over the seed / fruit.
- **BEST PULL:** this server only, new board every 10 minutes ("new board in 7:42"). Biggest Fruit Today is unchanged.
- **Trampolines** replace the benches and flower bed in the two garden nooks (about 30-stud bounce, ur boing sound 94320656351627), using ur asset
  12088629887 when it loads.
- Garden beds: invisible ramps on the raised edges, no jump needed. Garden fronts: no welcome mats or coloured strips.
- **Track gate:** the refresh wall fits the gate opening, the biome keys read upright. **Track walls can't be stood on** after a fling.
- **SPEED NEEDED signs** stay high above each keeper's spawn point (the secret keeper's at its spawn that time) and don't follow the keeper.
- **Holes:** 1.5x bigger, catch carriers at any speed, and the dirt no longer floats into the sky. The shovel tip says it also removes plants.
- Keyboard: keys click only for real steps (not flung, ragdolled or keepers); other players' landings click.
- Fast swoosh (102531686500130) when a keeper launches a player. Rare pack spawn notice uses ur new sound 80907337289683.
- Biome notifier: just "entering" + the biome name. Badges 1.5x, speed popups 2x, the whole treadmill belt scrolls.

### Packs and hotbar
- **Mech pack look B "Circuit Mech"**: gunmetal pouch, cyan traces, hex bolts, hazard seal, antenna LED; the opening backs out bolts, scans and
  steams. Same odds and price.
- **Pack parts fixed:** the Verity pack's seals sit on its body, tall packs keep their crimps, the Void pack's corner runes sit on the pouch.
- **Hotbar:** one press equips (packs used to wait up to 2.5 s for their shape and a second press put them away), **drag to reorder** on PC and
  phone (0.4 s hold on phone), the order is kept when u respawn. `/test hotbar` logs every press if one ever goes missing.

### Fixes, safety, smoothness, speed
- **Effects are smooth:** mutation and weather effects, track packs, held items, trails, keepers, Verity's hop, the hub items, garden effects,
  popups and belts all move every frame where u can see them (they stepped at 10–30 times a second).
- **If a server breaks while loading it kicks everyone** ("this server broke while loading 😭 pls rejoin") and anyone who joins it later. In
  Studio it only shows the message and the reason.

- **Free Void packs can't be gifted, all the way down:** the giveaway pack and the day-7 login Void pack, their seed, plant and fruit

  the hotbar ring stays still with Reduced Motion, a queued hotbar press can be cancelled and expires, the "ALMOST THERE!" wiggle never hides the ready pop.
- **Performance patch** (nothing u see or hear changes):

  - the Void giveaway pack costs a quarter (326 → 105 writes a frame at the plaza);
  - **73 unused scripts removed: 4.5 MB less to download for every player at join**;
