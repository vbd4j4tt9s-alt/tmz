# R136: Legendary and Mythic pulls, lantern lights, 1/N odds

Needs R135 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R136_install.lua` (~41 KB, 8 scripts, 1 of them new).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R136] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R136_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed
- **Legendary and Mythic seed pulls are a moment now.** Preview: `docs/proposals/R136/reveal_legendary_mythic.gif`.
  - **Charge-up:** the seed no longer pops straight out. Legendary waits 0.7 s and Mythic 1 s. The bag's mouth glows brighter and sparkles swirl down into it.
  - **Burst:**
    - a beam of light shoots up out of the bag;
    - a shockwave ring spreads out around it;
    - sparkles burst out, with a flash of light.
  - **Mythic is bigger:**
    - a taller beam;
    - two rings;
    - more sparkles;
    - a spiral of sparkles that climbs the beam;
    - pink and blue instead of gold.
  - **Sounds for you:** a rising whoosh during the charge-up, then rising chimes (one for Legendary, two for Mythic), then a hit and a bright chime when the seed bursts out.
  - **On your screen:** sparkles fly into the reveal, then a ring and a soft colour flash when the seed bursts out. The flash is skipped if Reduced Motion is on.
  - **Other players nearby** see the whole world effect and hear a soft sparkle sound at your bag.
  - Common to Rare, and Secret and up, look and sound the same as before.
  - The opening lasts 0.6 s longer for Legendary and 0.9 s longer for Mythic, so the pack stays in your hand until the seed is out.
- **Market lanterns:** the three ceiling lamps are now real glowing lantern fruits hanging from the cords, with the same warm light.
- **Every chance reads 1/N:**
  - the Index, Mech shop list and odds commands show `1/30`, `1/3.1` or `1/1.67B`, never `3.3%` or `23/600`;
  - chances better than 1 in 10 keep one decimal, like `1/3.1`.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R136'`.
