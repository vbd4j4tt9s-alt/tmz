# R130: performance pass, dark sky during refresh, gift popup

Needs R129 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R130_install.lua` (~44 KB, 7 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R130] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R130_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed

**Performance (no visual or gameplay change)**
- **Keepers standing at home:** the server used to fully re-prepare every idle keeper 4 times a second (all parts rewritten, a visibility scan, a re-pivot, 4 attributes).
  - Now a prepared keeper only gets cheap checks: back home if it moved, attributes written only when wrong.
  - It is fully re-prepared when its parts change, when it is replaced, after a chase, and every 5 s as a safety net.
  - Measured on the mock, 7 keepers over 20 s: 567 → 35 full prepares, 2,240 → 0 attribute writes, 240 → 0 re-pivots.
- **Movement check:** the server's anti-speed check reuses one raycast setup instead of making a new one 10 times a second per moving player. The refresh wall still blocks from the first check after it appears.
- Details and what was looked at but left alone: `docs/proposals/R130/PERF_REPORT.md`.

**Track refresh: the sky turns dark**
- Clients that can make the black sky image (as before): black sky.
- Clients that can't (Roblox's image API is off for many places and devices): the sky used to stay light. Now there is no blank sky box, the clock is midnight, and the air turns black like The Darkened's lights-out, at about the usual view distance. The sky and the far distance go dark; the base stays readable.
- During rain or snow, no grey weather cloud layer appears during a refresh.
- Everything comes back exactly when the refresh ends.

**Gifting**
- Hold a fruit, seed or pack and point at a player: they **light up gold** when they are close enough to receive it (mouse on computer, screen centre on controller).
- Click / tap / RT: a popup asks **"Are you sure you want to give *Frosted Golden Apple* to *Sunny*?"** with Give and Cancel (B or Esc also cancels). Nothing is sent until you press Give.
- The receiver gets a notice: **"🎁 *You* gave you *Frosted Golden Apple*!"** (fruit used to say only "A gifted crop was added to your bag").
- The giver sees "Gift sent to Sunny! (Frosted Golden Apple)". Too far away: "Get closer to Sunny to give."
- Previews: `docs/proposals/R130/gift_popup_1280x720.png` and `gift_popup_844x390.png` (approximate renders).

`Config.Version = 'V150 R130'`.
