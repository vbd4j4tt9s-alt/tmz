# R158l: your party commands (giant, give packs, pack rain, disco, server-wide sound)

Needs **R158k installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158k'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158l_install.lua` (~119 KB, 12 scripts,
5 new). Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for
**`[R158l] Installed.`**, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158l'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158l_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. No saved-data change.

## The commands (each one separate; all but givepack are for you only)
- **`/test giant [1-20|off] [@name|@all]`**: makes you (or them) giant. Default 5x. Walk speed stays the game's; jumps get bigger. Stays
  through respawns until `giant off` (or scale 1) or the player leaves. A giant can't reach Hold-E prompts (they're too high up).
- **`/test givepack <biome|rarity|pack name> [count] [@name|@all]`**: gives packs. Any order: `givepack storm mythic 3`,
  `givepack mythic 10 @all`, `givepack verity`. Names: void, verity, secret, cosmic, king, mech, roster. Up to 500. They are TEST packs
  (no announcement, no BEST PULL), like `/test pack`.
- **`/test packrain [count 1-500, default 30] [biome] [rarity] [seconds 5-120, default 20]`**, `packrain off`, `packrain status`:
  packs fall from the sky all over the map at random spots. Each is a random pack (unless you name one) at a random size from 0.6x to 2x
  (only the look; the pack you get is normal). **Touch one and it goes straight into your Bag.** One pack, one player. A pack nobody takes
  shrinks away **10 seconds** after it lands. Never more than 100 at once. Everyone sees "📦 PACK RAIN!". Full Bag: the pack stays there.
- **`/test disco [on|off|seconds|status]`**: a disco for the whole server: a spinning mirror ball over the hub, sweeping spotlights, a
  dance floor flashing to the beat, confetti and a colour tint. Players who join late see it too. Off puts the lighting back exactly as it
  was. Fast Mode and phones get a lighter version; Reduced Motion gets no flashing.
- **`/test sound <id> [volume 0-10] [loop]`**, `sound stop`: plays a sound everyone in the server hears (not from a spot; players who
  join while it plays hear it too). A new one replaces the old one; a non-looping one removes itself when it ends. Roblox only plays audio
  that's public or allowed for this game; if it can't load you get "That sound couldn't load (it may be private or not allowed in this game)."

`Config.Version = 'V150 R158l'`.

- `/test sound <a sound id you own>` then `/test sound stop`. **If a sound you know works always says "couldn't load", tell me** (the
