# R158m: pack opening animations always play in full (only the SKIP button skips)

Needs **R158l installed** (`Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158l'`). **One installer:** `installers/R158m_install.lua` (~44 KB, 4 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, **close every script tab first**, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter, wait for **`[R158m] Installed.`**,
then **Save** and start a **new** Play session. `Config.Version` reads `'V150 R158m'` after it.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R158m_Backup.Installer)("undo")`. No saved-data change.

## What was wrong
A click collected your seed 0.25 s after it first appeared, so spam-clicking cut off the rest of its animation (the seed floating, the
"1 in N", the end of the Secret / Cosmic / King scenes). Enter, gamepad B / R2 and opening a menu did the same.

## What changed
- **Every pack animation plays all the way through**, however fast you click or tap.
- **The SKIP button (bottom right) is the only skip.** Same as before: only on Secret, Cosmic and King, until the big hit.
- **Click to collect is the same**, but it works once the animation has finished. "click to collect!" / "tap to collect!" shows up at that
  moment (not before, while a click still did nothing).
- Dying, getting ragdolled, a chase or being teleported still collect your seed at once: you never lose it.
- A pack you switch to during an animation is blocked from the very first frame (it used to be one frame late; that could never open a
  pack, but now it can't count a click either).

When you can collect (seconds after the pack opens): Common 1.45, Uncommon 1.75, Rare 2.25, Legendary 3.75, Mythic 4.75, Secret 5.35,
Cosmic 6.85, King 8.95.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R158m'`.
