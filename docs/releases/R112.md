## What changed (37 scripts, 5 of them new)

- **Balance:** King 1 in 1 trillion in a basic pack. Boots Sand x50, Frost x500, Lava x20K, Crystal x1M, **Thunder x50M**
  (Electric Boots renamed). Thunder: a King about 1 in 1,000 packs. Packs players already hold keep their old odds.
  Keeper speeds x1.6 per biome; new speed curve (first minute front-loaded). Full tables: `docs/proposals/R111_balancing_spec.md`.
- **No base slow zone:** everyone moves at full speed in the base, gardens and market (new players 24, was 9.6).
- **Boots:** biome materials (reflective glass crystal, ice + snow fur, basalt + magma, sandstone + leather, metal + bolts),
  footprints and bursts while walking, idle effect when standing still, for every pair. Budgeted for crowds and low graphics.
- **Inventory + hotbar:** 3D picture of every pack, seed, fruit and tool (bat too), small name, weight in kg, "xN" stacks.
- **Weights in kg** instead of "2x" (inventory, sell menu, pack notices).
- **Planting:** cube dirt pile that pops up around the seed (bigger plant = bigger pile), sinks after ~2.4 s, leaves a mark;
  layered thud/crunch/pop sound (ids at the top of `ReplicatedStorage.PlantingEffects` - swap in Creator Store sounds).
- **Bat swing** fixed (it played backwards) and **keeper strikes**: wind back, hold, slap forward, follow through.
  Hit timing, reach and damage are unchanged.
- **Refresh night wall:** white wall, outlined moon + clouds, big "Ns" countdown.
- **Rare pack notices:** only Legendary and Mythic packs.
- **UI:** speed number jumps when it goes up; "FASTER! N run speed" text removed; tutorial guide name TMZ.
- `Config.Version = 'V149 R112'`.
