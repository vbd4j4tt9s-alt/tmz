# R139: rainbow ring on new packs, the free tutorial pack is a secret one-time thing

Needs R138 installed first (refuses otherwise, changing nothing). Installer: `installers/R139_install.lua` (~31 KB, 6 scripts).
Edit mode, Play stopped, paste ALL of it into the Command Bar, Enter.
**Wait for `[R139] Installed.`** before pressing Play, then **Save** and start a **new** Play session.

Undo: `require(game.ServerStorage.ChestChase_R139_Backup.Installer)("undo")`.

## What changed
### New packs get a rainbow ring in your inventory
- **Which packs:** any pack you get while playing: a steal, a gift, the treadmill bonus, an event pack, a Mech pack, the free tutorial pack.
  - The packs you already had when you joined never glow.
  - Each pack glows only once. Respawning doesn't light it up again.
- **What you see:**
  - a **rainbow border** with its colours spinning slowly around the edge;
  - it's on the pack's **hotbar slot**, and on its card in the **Bag**;
  - a new pack that joins a stack (x3) lights up that stack.
- **How long:**
  - The 4 seconds only start once you can **see** it. A pack that's only in the Bag (hotbar full) waits until you open the Bag.
  - It stays bright for **4 s**, then **fades out over 2 s** and is gone.
  - A pack you never look at stops waiting after 10 minutes.
- **Reduced Motion:** the colours stay still instead of spinning.
- **Performance:** it only runs while something is glowing, and turns itself off when nothing is.

### The free tutorial pack: one time, and secret
- **It's still 2x luck.** Only the server knows that.
- **Players can't tell:**
  - it's called and looks exactly like any **Forest Seed Pack**;
  - holding it shows the **normal Forest odds** (no 🍀 2x LUCK row, no doubled chances);
  - the notice says "🎁 FREE Forest Seed Pack! Check your Bag!";
  - the finish card says "🎁 FREE PACK!";
  - nothing in the tutorial mentions luck or 2x.
- **A one-time thing:**
  - one per account, ever. Replaying the tutorial never gives another;
  - the luck is for the player who earned it. **Gifting it hands over a normal Forest pack.** That also stops alts farming lucky packs for a main account. It covers gifts sent before R139 that haven't arrived yet.

`Config.Version = 'V150 R139'`.

## Test in Studio
- **Rainbow ring:**
  - Steal a pack and bank it. Its hotbar slot gets the spinning rainbow border for about 4 s, then it fades.
  - Your other packs stay plain, and so do they after you reset your character.
  - Fill the hotbar, get another pack, wait a bit, then open the Bag: its card glows then.
- **Free pack** (Settings → replay the tutorial on an account that hasn't had it yet; in Studio a fresh save does):
  - the finish card says 🎁 FREE PACK!, the notice says FREE Forest Seed Pack;
  - hold it: the tooltip matches a normal Forest pack's (no luck row).
  - Replay again: no second pack.
  - Gift it to an alt: it arrives as a normal Forest pack.
